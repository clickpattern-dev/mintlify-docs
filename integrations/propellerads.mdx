### What the integration does

| Feature | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Cost sync | Spend, impressions and clicks for campaigns and zones |
| Campaign import | Your PropellerAds campaigns |
| PatternPilot actions | **Pause campaign**, **Resume campaign**, **Adjust bid**, **Adjust budget** |

PatternPilot acts on whole campaigns. Zone-level and banner-level actions aren't available in rules.

### What you need

- Your PropellerAds API token.

### Connect PropellerAds

1. Go to **Data Hub → Integrations**.
2. Under **Available Integrations**, click the **PropellerAds** card.
3. Fill in the fields below and click **Integrate**.

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **API Token** | Your PropellerAds API token. Required. |
| **Assign to workspace** | The workspace that receives the integration. |
| **Custom account name** | Optional name for this connection. |
| **Auto-matching for all campaigns** | On by default. Leave it on. |

You see **PropellerAds Connected!** when it works.

### How bid and budget changes work

- **Adjust bid** sets the new amount on every active rate of the campaign. Each rate keeps its countries.
- If the campaign has no active rates, the bid change fails. Nothing is changed.
- **Adjust budget** changes the campaign's daily or total spend limit.
- **Pause campaign** stops the campaign. **Resume campaign** starts it again.

### Manage the connection

Click the PropellerAds card under **Connected Integrations**. The detail page has **Performance**, **Campaigns**, **Campaign Mapping** and **Sync Logs** tabs, plus **Sync Now** and **Delete**.

### FAQ

**My campaign has different bids per country. Will a rule flatten them?** Yes. **Adjust bid** applies the same amount to every active rate, though each rate keeps its country list.

**My token stopped working. How do I update it?** Delete the integration on its detail page, then connect PropellerAds again with the new token.
