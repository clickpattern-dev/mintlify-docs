### What the integration does

| Feature | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Cost sync | Spend, clicks, impressions and conversions for campaigns and ad groups |
| Campaign import | Campaigns and ad groups |
| CAPI | Conversion uploads to your Google Ads conversion actions, including refund adjustments from Shopify |
| PatternPilot actions | **Pause**, **Resume**, **Adjust budget**, and **Adjust bid** on ad groups with manual CPC bidding |

### Connect Google Ads

1. Go to **Data Hub → Integrations**.
2. Click the **Google Ads** card.
3. Sign in with Google and approve access.
4. In **Configure Google Ads Integration**, select the account to sync.
5. Click **Save and Start Sync**.

The account can't be changed after setup. To use a different account, delete this integration and connect again.

### Manager (MCC) accounts

If your Google login manages client accounts, the list shows each manager as a heading, for example "Agency (Manager Account - 3 clients)", with its client accounts below. Select a client account. You can't select the manager itself.

If a manager account has no linked clients, you see **Manager Account Detected**. Google doesn't allow campaign metrics from a manager account directly. Either link or create a client account under the manager, or connect a regular Google Ads account instead.

### The Google Ads integration page

Tabs: **Performance**, **Campaigns**, **Ad Groups**, **Campaign Mapping**, **CAPI**, **Sync Logs** and **Health**.

The **Ad Groups** tab lists ad group performance for the last 30 days: **Ad Group Name**, **Campaign**, **Status**, **Cost**, **Clicks**, **Impressions**, **Conversions** and **CTR**. Filter by **Campaign** when you have more than one.

### Pass the ad group ID

ClickPattern maps Google ad groups to your campaigns automatically when visits carry the ad group ID. Add Google's `{adgroupid}` ValueTrack parameter to your tracking template. Google adds the `gclid` click ID itself when auto-tagging is on.

### Upload conversions to Google (CAPI)

Google Ads CAPI uploads ClickPattern conversions to conversion actions in your account.

1. Open the **CAPI** tab and click **Set Up CAPI**.
2. Select the **Google Ads account**.
3. Under **Which conversion action each event is sent to**, pick a conversion action for each event. Use **Add an event** to add more rows.
4. Optional: turn on **Email and phone matching (enhanced conversions for leads)**.
5. Click **Save & Enable**.

Only events with a conversion action are sent. Events left without one show **Won't be sent to Google**.

Only enabled conversion actions of type `UPLOAD_CLICKS` that don't use external attribution appear in the list. If the account has none, create one in Google Ads first. Open **How to create the conversion action in Google Ads** in the setup window for step-by-step instructions. A new action can take up to 6 hours before Google accepts uploads.

### Enhanced conversions for leads

With **Email and phone matching** on, ClickPattern sends each conversion's email and phone, hashed, so Google can match conversions without a click ID. Google accepts them only after your Google Ads account has accepted the customer data terms and turned on enhanced conversions for leads (Google Ads → Goals → Conversions → Settings). With it off, conversions match by Google click ID only, and a conversion without one isn't sent.

### Upload timing

Google accepts a conversion only once its click is at least 6 hours old. ClickPattern holds each conversion and uploads it about 6 hours after the click. Until then, the delivery log shows **Scheduled**.

### Refunds

Google Ads is the only platform that receives refunds through CAPI. Shopify refunds and cancellations retract the conversion (full refund) or restate it with the new total (partial refund).

### Bids: ad groups only, manual CPC only

Google sets bids on ad groups, not campaigns. ClickPattern changes CPC bids only:

- A bid change at campaign level is refused.
- A bid change is refused when the campaign uses an automated bidding strategy, because Google would ignore it. Switch the campaign to manual CPC (or enhanced CPC) to control the bid.
- CPM and CPA bid changes aren't supported.

To use **Adjust bid** in PatternPilot, map the ClickPattern campaign to a Google ad group. Budget changes work at campaign level.

### Token renewal

Google access refreshes automatically. If it expires, the page shows "Your Google access token has expired. Reconnect to resume syncing." Click **Reconnect**.

### FAQ

**Why can't I select my MCC account?** Google doesn't return campaign metrics for manager accounts. Select one of its client accounts.

**Why is my conversion action missing from the list?** Only enabled `UPLOAD_CLICKS` actions without external attribution can receive uploads. Check the action's type, status and attribution in Google Ads.

**My bid rule ran but nothing changed in Google.** Check that the rule targets an ad group mapped to the campaign, and that the Google campaign uses manual CPC bidding.

**Is there a test event for Google Ads?** No. Google has no test mode, so every upload is a real conversion. Check the delivery logs instead.
