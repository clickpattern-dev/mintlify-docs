### What the integration does

| Feature | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Cost sync | Spend, impressions and clicks per campaign |
| Campaign import | Campaigns that had traffic in the last 30 days |
| PatternPilot actions | **Pause campaign**, **Resume campaign**, **Adjust bid**, **Adjust budget** |

PatternPilot acts on whole campaigns. Zone-level actions aren't available in rules.

### What you need

- Your Clickadu API token.

### Connect Clickadu

1. Go to **Data Hub → Integrations**.
2. Under **Available Integrations**, click the **Clickadu** card.
3. Fill in the fields below and click **Integrate**.

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **API Token** | Your Clickadu API token. Required. |
| **Assign to workspace** | The workspace that receives the integration. |
| **Custom account name** | Optional name for this connection. |
| **Auto-matching for all campaigns** | On by default. Leave it on. |

### How actions work on Clickadu

- **Pause campaign** sets the campaign to Stopped in Clickadu. **Resume campaign** sets it back to Working.
- **Adjust bid** applies the new amount to every country rate on the campaign.
- **Adjust budget** changes the campaign's daily or total amount.
- If Clickadu's campaign data lacks the rate or budget field, the change fails and nothing is written.

### Manage the connection

Click the Clickadu card under **Connected Integrations**. The detail page has **Performance**, **Campaigns**, **Campaign Mapping** and **Sync Logs** tabs, plus **Sync Now** and **Delete**.

### FAQ

**Why is one of my Clickadu campaigns missing?** ClickPattern finds Clickadu campaigns through their recent statistics. A campaign with no traffic in the last 30 days doesn't appear.

**Why are all imported campaigns shown as active?** Clickadu's statistics don't include campaign status, so imported campaigns show as active. Check the real status in Clickadu.

**Why did a paused campaign show as Stopped in Clickadu?** ClickPattern pauses Clickadu campaigns by setting them to Stopped. Resume sets them back to Working.
