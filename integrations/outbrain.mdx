### What the integration does

| Feature | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Cost sync | Spend, impressions and clicks for your campaigns and publishers |
| Campaign import | Your Outbrain campaigns and promoted links |
| PatternPilot actions | **Pause campaign**, **Resume campaign**, **Adjust bid**, **Adjust budget** |

PatternPilot acts on whole campaigns. Publisher-level and section-level actions aren't available in rules.

### What you need

- The email and password you use to sign in to Outbrain Amplify.
- If your login can see more than one Outbrain account, the name (or ID) of the account you want to sync.

### Connect Outbrain

1. Go to **Data Hub → Integrations**.
2. Under **Available Integrations**, click the **Outbrain** card.
3. Fill in the fields below and click **Integrate**.

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Login/Email** | Your Outbrain Amplify account login email. Required. |
| **Password** | Your Outbrain Amplify account password. Required. |
| **Outbrain account name** | Optional. An account name copied from your Outbrain account. Fill it in if you have multiple users related to one Outbrain account. You can enter the account's name or ID. |
| **Assign to workspace** | The workspace that receives the integration. |
| **Custom account name** | Optional name for this connection. |
| **Auto-matching for all campaigns** | On by default. Leave it on. |

If your login has access to several marketer accounts, enter **Outbrain account name** when you connect. Without it, ClickPattern can't tell which account to use and doesn't create the integration.

### Choose or change the marketer account

The detail page has three tabs: **Configuration**, **Performance** and **Campaigns**.

1. Click the Outbrain card under **Connected Integrations**.
2. Open the **Configuration** tab.
3. Select an account in **Marketer Account** and click **Save**.

If no account is set, the page shows **Marketer Account Not Configured**. Campaigns and cost don't sync until you pick one.

### Map Outbrain campaigns

The Outbrain detail page has no mapping tab. Use **Unmapped objects** on the **Integrations** page to link Outbrain campaigns to ClickPattern campaigns.

### FAQ

**I get "Rate limit exceeded. Please wait a few minutes and try again."** Outbrain allows very few logins per hour. Wait before you try again, and avoid repeated connect attempts.

**I get "The specified Outbrain account was not found on this login."** The value in **Outbrain account name** doesn't match any account on your login. Copy the name exactly as Outbrain shows it, or use the account ID.

**I get "No marketer accounts found for this Outbrain user".** Your login has no advertising access. Ask the Outbrain account owner to grant it.

**Can I sync two Outbrain marketer accounts?** Each integration syncs one marketer account. Change it on the **Configuration** tab.
