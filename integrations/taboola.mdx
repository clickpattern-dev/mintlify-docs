### What the integration does

| Feature | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Cost sync | Spend, impressions and clicks for your campaigns and sites |
| Campaign import | Your Taboola campaigns and ads |
| PatternPilot actions | **Pause campaign**, **Resume campaign**, **Adjust bid**, **Adjust budget** |

PatternPilot acts on whole campaigns. Site-level actions aren't available in rules.

### What you need

- Your Taboola **Client ID** and **Client Secret**. If you don't have them, ask your Taboola account manager.
- Advertiser access to at least one Taboola account on those credentials.

### Connect Taboola

1. Go to **Data Hub → Integrations**.
2. Under **Available Integrations**, find **Taboola**. Use **Search by name** or set **Category** to **Traffic source**.
3. Click the Taboola card. The **Integrate with Taboola** dialog opens.
4. Fill in the fields below and click **Integrate**.
5. If Taboola returns more than one account, an **Ad account** list appears. Select the account and click **Integrate** again.

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Client ID** | Your Taboola Client ID. Required. |
| **Client Secret** | Your Taboola Client Secret. Required. |
| **Assign to workspace** | The workspace that receives the integration. |
| **Custom account name** | Optional name for this connection. Defaults to "Taboola". |
| **Auto-matching for all campaigns** | On by default. Leave it on. |

When the connection succeeds, you see **Taboola Connected!** and the card moves to **Connected Integrations**. The first sync starts right away.

If ClickPattern can't list your accounts, a **Taboola account ID** field appears instead. Enter the Account ID shown in Taboola. ClickPattern checks that your credentials can reach that account before it connects.

### Manage the connection

Click the Taboola card under **Connected Integrations** to open its detail page. It shows **Status**, **Last Sync** and **Total Campaigns**, plus these tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Performance** | Impressions, clicks, CTR, cost, CPM and CPC for the last 7, 30 or 90 days |
| **Campaigns** | Imported Taboola campaigns with their IDs and status |
| **Campaign Mapping** | Which ClickPattern campaign each Taboola campaign is linked to |
| **Sync Logs** | Recent sync runs and errors |

Taboola has no ad set level, so you map each Taboola campaign directly to a ClickPattern campaign. Click **Sync Now** to sync immediately, or **Delete** to remove the integration.

### FAQ

**I get "Invalid Taboola credentials". What now?** Check that you copied the Client ID and Client Secret exactly. They must belong to the same Taboola API client.

**I get "No advertiser accounts found for these Taboola credentials".** Your credentials work, but they don't have advertiser access. Ask your Taboola account manager to grant it, then connect again.

**I get "That Taboola account is not on these credentials".** The account you chose or entered isn't available to this Client ID. Pick an account from the list instead.

**Why doesn't a rule's action reach Taboola?** PatternPilot can only act on Taboola campaigns that are mapped to a ClickPattern campaign. Check the **Campaign Mapping** tab.
