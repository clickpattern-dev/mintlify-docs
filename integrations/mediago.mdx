### What the integration does

| Feature | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Cost sync | Spend, impressions and clicks per campaign |
| Campaign import | Your MediaGo campaigns |
| PatternPilot actions | **Pause campaign**, **Resume campaign** |

MediaGo's API can't change a bid or budget on its own, so **Adjust bid** and **Adjust budget** don't work for MediaGo. Change those in MediaGo.

### What you need

- Your MediaGo API token. If you don't have one, ask your MediaGo account manager.

### Connect MediaGo

1. Go to **Data Hub → Integrations**.
2. Under **Available Integrations**, click the **MediaGo** card.
3. Fill in the fields below and click **Integrate**.

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **API Token** | Your MediaGo API token. Required. |
| **Assign to workspace** | The workspace that receives the integration. |
| **Custom account name** | Optional name for this connection. |
| **Auto-matching for all campaigns** | On by default. Leave it on. |

### Manage the connection

Click the MediaGo card under **Connected Integrations**. The detail page has **Performance**, **Campaigns**, **Campaign Mapping** and **Sync Logs** tabs, plus **Sync Now** and **Delete**.

### FAQ

**Can rules change MediaGo bids or budgets?** No. Rules can pause and resume MediaGo campaigns only.

**Why don't I see cost for some days?** MediaGo reports only campaigns that spent money in the period. A campaign with no spend has no cost rows.
