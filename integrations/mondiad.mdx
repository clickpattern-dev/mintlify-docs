### What the integration does

| Feature | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Cost sync | Spend, impressions and clicks per campaign |
| Campaign import | Your Mondiad campaigns |
| PatternPilot actions | None |

Mondiad only accepts campaign updates that resend every campaign setting, including all targeting. A partial change could overwrite your targeting, so ClickPattern doesn't pause, resume or change bids or budgets on Mondiad. Make those changes in Mondiad.

### What you need

- Your Mondiad Client ID and Client Secret.

### Connect Mondiad

1. Go to **Data Hub → Integrations**.
2. Under **Available Integrations**, click the **Mondiad** card.
3. Fill in the fields below and click **Integrate**.

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Client ID** | Your Mondiad Client ID. Required. |
| **Client Secret** | Your Mondiad Client Secret. Required. |
| **Assign to workspace** | The workspace that receives the integration. |
| **Custom account name** | Optional name for this connection. |
| **Auto-matching for all campaigns** | On by default. Leave it on. |

### Manage the connection

Click the Mondiad card under **Connected Integrations**. The detail page has **Performance**, **Campaigns**, **Campaign Mapping** and **Sync Logs** tabs, plus **Sync Now** and **Delete**.

### FAQ

**Can I build PatternPilot rules on Mondiad campaigns?** You can use rules that send alerts or notifications. Rules that pause, resume or change bids or budgets do nothing on Mondiad.

**Which currency is Mondiad cost in?** Mondiad reports spend in USD.
