### What a campaign holds

- **Workspace**: the Private workspace the campaign belongs to.
- **Traffic source**: where the visitors come from. Its parameters are added to the campaign URL.
- **Destination**: where visitors go, either a saved flow or a path set up on the campaign itself.
- **Cost tracking**: how ClickPattern knows what you paid for the traffic. See Cost models.
- Labels: **Campaign name**, **Country label**, **Traffic type** (Desktop, Mobile, Tablet or Mixed), **Tags** and **Notes**.

After you save, the form switches to the **Tracking & PatternPilot** tab, which shows the campaign URL to put in your ad. To see it later, open the campaign with **Edit**.

For redirect tracking the campaign URL looks like `https://{domain}/c/7d2f4a10-9c3e-4b8a-a1f2-5e6d7c8b9a01` followed by your traffic source parameters.

### Simple vs Advanced form

Both forms are under **Create** on **Performance → Campaigns**.

|  | Campaign Simple | Campaign Advanced |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tabs | **General**, **Tracking & PatternPilot** | **GENERAL**, **DESTINATION**, **TRACKING & PATTERNPILOT** |
| Path destination | Pick landers and offers in a list | Full path editor with default paths and rule-based paths |
| Weights | Spread evenly when you save | Set your own |
| Flow tools | Pick an existing flow | Pick a flow, **Load from flow template**, or **Save as flow** |
| Tracking method | Set by **Transition to offer** | **Redirect** or **Direct** under **Transition between ad and campaign funnel** |

Use the Advanced form when you need custom weights or rules. You can switch a simple campaign with **Convert to Advanced Campaign**. This is one way: once converted, the campaign can only be edited in the Advanced form.

### Destination types

Under **Campaign destination** you choose **Flow** or **Path**.

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Flow** | Uses a saved flow. Edit the flow once and every campaign that uses it follows. |
| **Path** | Sets the route on this campaign only. |

A path sends visitors one of two ways:

| Path destination | Visitor journey |
| --- | --- |
| **Landers & Offers** | Campaign URL → lander → offer. The lander's CTA links send the visitor on to an offer. |
| **Offers only** | Campaign URL → offer. No landing page. |

All offers in one campaign must use the same tracking domain. Saving fails with "All offers in a campaign must use the same tracking domain" otherwise.

### Statuses and archiving

| Status | New visits | Conversions from earlier clicks | How to change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Active | Redirected normally | Recorded | **Actions → Resume** |
| Paused | Stopped right away. The campaign URL no longer redirects. | Still recorded | **Actions → Pause** |
| Archived | Stopped. The campaign stops accepting clicks. | Still recorded | **Actions → Archive** |

**Unarchive** restores a campaign as paused. Resume it separately to start traffic again.

### FAQ

**Can I change a campaign's traffic source later?** Yes, edit the campaign. The campaign URL's parameters come from the traffic source, so copy the URL again from the **Tracking & PatternPilot** tab and update your ad.

**Why can't I see the campaign URL while creating a campaign?** The **Tracking & PatternPilot** tab unlocks once the campaign is saved. After you click **Create Campaign**, the form switches to it. Later, open it with **Edit**.

**Can I go back from an advanced campaign to the simple form?** No. Converting to advanced is permanent for that campaign.
