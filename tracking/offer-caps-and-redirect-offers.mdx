---
title: "Offer caps and redirect offers"
description: "A cap limits how many clicks or conversions an offer gets per day. When the cap is hit, new visitors go to a redirect offer you choose."
---

### Before you start

You need at least one other offer in the same workspace. Until then, **Enable offer cap** is disabled and you'll see: "You need to create at least one other offer in this workspace to enable offer capping."

### Steps

1. Open the offer: go to **Performance → Offers**, select it and click **Edit**. Or create a new one.
2. Scroll to **Offer cap** and tick **Enable offer cap**.
3. Under **Cap type**, pick **Conversion cap** or **Click cap**.
4. Enter the **Daily cap**.
5. Pick a **Time zone** for the daily reset.
6. Pick a **Redirect offer**. To make one now, choose **Create a new offer** at the bottom of the list.
7. Click **Update Offer** (or **Create Offer**).

### Field reference

| Field | What to enter | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Enable offer cap** | Turns capping on | Off |
| **Cap type** | **Conversion cap** counts conversions. **Click cap** counts visitors sent to the offer. | None |
| **Daily cap** | A whole number of 1 or more | Empty |
| **Time zone** | When the day starts and ends for the count. **None** uses UTC. | None |
| **Redirect offer** | Where new visitors go after the cap is reached | None |

### How capping works

- Counts reset at midnight in the offer's time zone.
- Once the cap is reached, new visitors go to the redirect offer.
- With **Conversion cap**, conversions above the cap on the same day are rejected.
- If the redirect offer is also capped, or no redirect offer is set, the visitor gets a "Page not found" page.
- Redirects don't chain. The redirect offer's own redirect offer is not used.

### FAQ

**Can I cap without a redirect offer?** Yes, but capped traffic is then lost. Visitors see "Page not found" until the day resets.

**Do caps work on lander CTAs?** Yes. Clicks from a lander to a capped offer also go to the redirect offer.

**Why is Enable offer cap greyed out?** Pick a workspace first, and make sure it has at least one other offer.
