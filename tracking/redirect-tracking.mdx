### Campaign URL format

```
https://{domain}/c/{campaignId}?{traffic source parameters}
```

Example: `https://{domain}/c/7d2f4a10-3b9c-4e21-9f6a-1c2d3e4f5a6b?zone={zoneid}&cost={cost}`

- `{campaignId}` is the campaign's UUID.
- The parameters come from your traffic source's enabled parameter rows.
- Copy the full URL from the campaign's **Tracking & PatternPilot** tab.

### What happens on each click

1. ClickPattern finds the campaign.
2. Traffic filtering rules run. Blocked visitors go to a notice page.
3. The flow or path picks a lander or offer.
4. The visit is recorded with its click ID, device, location and parameters.
5. The visitor is forwarded using the transition mode.

### Paused or archived campaigns

- New clicks on a paused or archived campaign are not tracked or forwarded.
- The visitor gets a "Gone" response (HTTP 410).
- Conversions from earlier clicks are still recorded.

### Error responses

| Response | Cause |
| --- | --- |
| 400 "Invalid campaign slug" | The ID in the URL isn't a valid campaign ID. Check for a cut-off link. |
| 404 "Campaign not found" | No campaign has that ID. |
| 404 "Page not found" | The offer is capped and has no available redirect offer. |
| 500 "No destination configured" | The campaign has no landers or offers configured. |

### Filtered traffic page

Visitors blocked by traffic filtering see a notice page with no link to your offer. The title depends on the reason:

| Title | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| **Content Not Available in Your Region** | Location restriction |
| **Device Not Supported** | Device restriction |
| **Operating System Not Supported** | OS restriction |
| **Content Not Available at This Time** | Day or hour restriction |
| **Content Not Available** | Any other reason, including your own Traffic filtering rules |

Each page ends with "If you believe this is an error, please contact the advertiser." Filtered visits are still recorded.

### FAQ

**Can I change the domain in the campaign URL?** Yes. Pick another domain under the preview selector on the **Tracking & PatternPilot** tab and copy that URL. Use the same domain for your lander click URLs.

**Why does my paused campaign show "Gone"?** Paused campaigns stop forwarding traffic. Resume the campaign to send visitors again.

**Do I need a separate URL for each ad?** No. Use one campaign URL with your traffic source's tokens. The tokens tell ads and placements apart.
