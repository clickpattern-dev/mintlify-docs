### Types of domain

| Type | Badge in Settings → Domains | Who uses it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shared platform domain | **Platform**, **Shared** | Part of a trial pool; may be used by other ClickPattern accounts |
| Dedicated platform domain | **Platform**, **Dedicated** | Dedicated to your account |
| Custom domain | **Custom** | Your own domain, added under **Custom Domains** |

Platform domains are ready to use immediately. For an exclusive domain on a shared setup, add a custom domain.

### Public vs Restricted

Visibility applies to custom domains.

| Visibility | Available to |
| --- | --- |
| **Public** (default) | All workspaces on your account, including new ones |
| **Restricted** | Only the workspaces you choose |

A domain's row shows **All workspaces** when Public, or the assigned workspace names when Restricted. Use **All**, **Public** and **Restricted** above the list to filter.

### Change a domain's visibility

1. Go to **Settings → Domains**.
2. Click the ⋮ menu on the domain and choose **Edit visibility**.
3. Select **Public — available to all workspaces** or **Restricted — selected workspaces only**.
4. For Restricted, pick workspaces in **Assign to workspaces**. At least one is required.
5. Click **Save**.

### Remove from a workspace vs delete

| Action | Where | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Remove from this workspace** | ⋮ menu, Restricted domains only | Removed from the current workspace. It stays on your account, and other workspaces keep using it. |
| **Delete domain** | ⋮ menu | Deleted from your whole account and every workspace. Its name is released. This can't be undone. |

Before you delete a domain, move campaigns that use it to another domain. Links on a deleted domain stop working.

### FAQ

**I can't see a domain in my campaign's domain list. Why?** It may be Restricted to other workspaces. Ask an admin to add your workspace under **Edit visibility**.

**Can I make a Restricted domain Public again?** Yes. Use **Edit visibility** and select Public.

**Why don't I see the ⋮ menu?** Changing domains needs edit permission in the current workspace.
