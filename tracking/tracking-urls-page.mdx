### Before you start

You need at least one tracking domain that is ready to use: a platform domain, or a custom domain that has passed verification. Without one, the page shows **No Verified Domains**. Go to **Settings → Domains** to add and verify a domain.

### Pick a tracking domain

Each section has a **Tracking Domain** selector. Platform domains carry a **Platform** badge. Verified custom domains carry **Verified** or **Cloudflare**.

Use the same domain everywhere in one funnel. The domain in your click URLs and conversion pixels must match the domain in the campaign URL exactly.

### Click URLs

This section is labeled **Click URL** on the page. Click URLs send visitors from your landing page to an offer. Paste one into the link of each call-to-action button on the lander.

| Field | Format | Use it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Click URL** | `https://{domain}/click` | The lander leads to one offer |
| **Multi-offer click URL** | `https://{domain}/click/1` | The lander has several buttons, each for a different offer. Use `/click/2`, `/click/3` and so on up to `/click/10`. |

Landing pages with a click URL don't need any other script to track clicks or send visitors to offers.

### Postback URLs

This section is labeled **Postback URL** on the page. A server-to-server postback is the most reliable way to count conversions. The affiliate network calls the URL when a conversion happens, so cookies and browsers don't matter.

1. Pick your **Tracking Domain**.
2. Optional: turn on **Include additional parameters in the postback URL** to add `param1`–`param5`.
3. Optional: choose a **Conversion event**. Pick a standard event or one of your custom conversions, or type the macro your network fills in, such as `{goal}`.
4. Copy **Postback URL** (`http://`) or **Secure postback URL** (`https://`).
5. Replace each `REPLACE` and `OPTIONAL` with your network's macros, then paste the URL into your network.

The basic URL looks like this:

`https://{domain}/postback?cid=REPLACE&et=OPTIONAL&payout=OPTIONAL&currency=OPTIONAL&txid=OPTIONAL`

No change needed if the shared with-params template fix ships. If it doesn't: With **Include additional parameters** on, the URL keeps `currency` and adds `param1=OPTIONAL` through `param5=OPTIONAL`. Add `payout` and `txid` yourself.

If you leave **Conversion event** empty, `et=OPTIONAL` stays in the URL for you to replace. Without an event type, the conversion is recorded untyped and sent to ad platforms as Purchase.

Make sure your offer URL includes the `{clickid}` token, for example `http://my_offer_URL.com/?aff_net_parameter={clickid}`. Otherwise conversions can't be registered.

### Conversion pixels

This section is labeled **Conversion Tracking Pixel** on the page. Use a pixel when an offer only supports cookie-based tracking. You paste it on the thank-you page.

1. Pick your **Tracking Domain**.
2. Optional: turn on **Include additional parameters in the conversion tracking pixel** to add `param1`–`param5`.
3. Choose a **Conversion event**. It is required: **Copy** stays disabled until you choose one.
4. Copy one of the three snippets.

| Snippet | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Conversion tracking pixel URL** | The pixel address only, for tools that ask for a URL: `https://{domain}/conversion.gif?et=lead` |
| **Conversion tracking pixel** | A 1x1 image tag to paste into the page body |
| **Conversion tracking script** | A JavaScript snippet that also finds the click ID automatically. Click **Expand** to see all of it. |

### FAQ

**Why is Copy greyed out in the pixel section?** Choose a **Conversion event** first. A thank-you page always records the same event, and nobody fills it in after you copy.

**Do I have to replace every `OPTIONAL`?** No. ClickPattern ignores values left as `OPTIONAL` or `REPLACE`. Replace them when you want that data recorded.

**Why doesn't my domain appear in the list?** Custom domains appear only after they pass verification. Check the domain's status in **Settings → Domains**.
