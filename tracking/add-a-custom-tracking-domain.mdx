### Why use one

- Links show your brand instead of a shared domain.
- The conversion cookie works when the tracking domain and your site share a main domain.
- Your tracking isn't affected by other accounts' traffic.

### Step 1: Add the domain

1. Go to **Settings → Domains**.
2. Under **Custom Domains**, enter your subdomain in **Add Custom Domain**, for example `track.yourbrand.com`. Root domains are not recommended.
3. Click **Add Domain**.

New domains are Public: available to all workspaces. See **Domains across workspaces** to restrict one.

### Step 2: Create the CNAME record

After you add the domain, a table of DNS records appears under it. It has **Type**, **Name**, **Value** and **Status** columns.

1. Copy the record with the copy icon.
2. Sign in to your DNS provider and create a CNAME record with that **Name** and **Value**.
3. Save the record.

There is one routing CNAME per domain.

If your DNS is on Cloudflare, set the record's proxy status to **DNS only** (grey cloud). A proxied (orange cloud) record hides the CNAME, and the domain can't be validated.

### Step 3: Wait for verification

Verification is automatic; you don't need to click **Verify**. Pending domains are checked every hour. DNS changes can take up to 48 hours to spread.

When the record is in place, an SSL certificate is issued automatically. The domain then shows **Verified — Cloudflare is serving this domain with a valid SSL certificate. No DNS records needed anymore.**

### Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Waiting for the DNS record or the certificate |
| **Partial** | Some checks passed, others haven't yet |
| **Verified** | Ready to use |
| **Failed** | A check failed. The error shows under the domain. |
| **Expired** | The SSL certificate has expired |

Each DNS record also shows **Passed**, **Pending** or **Error**.

### Error messages when adding a domain

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| `Invalid domain format` | Enter a hostname only, such as `track.yourbrand.com`, without `https://` or a path |
| `Domain already exists in this workspace` | It's already added; find it in the list |
| `This domain is already in use in workspace "…"` | Remove it from that workspace first, or use another subdomain |
| `This domain is a platform domain and cannot be registered as custom` | Choose a domain you own |
| `ClickPattern-owned domains cannot be registered as custom domains` | Choose a domain you own |

If you reach your plan's limit, the page shows **You've reached your plan's custom-domain limit.** See [pricing](https://www.clickpattern.com/pricing) for plan details.

### FAQ

**Can I use my root domain, such as `yourbrand.com`?** It isn't recommended. Use a subdomain so your website isn't affected.

**How long does verification take?** Usually minutes after the record is live. DNS changes can take up to 48 hours.

**Do I need to buy an SSL certificate?** No. SSL is issued and renewed automatically.
