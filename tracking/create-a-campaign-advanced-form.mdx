### Steps

1. Go to **Performance → Campaigns** and click **Create → Campaign Advanced**.
2. On **GENERAL**, fill in the campaign details and cost, choose **Redirect** or **Direct**, then click **Next**.
3. On **DESTINATION**, choose **Flow** or **Path** and build the destination.
4. Click **Save**. The form opens **TRACKING & PATTERNPILOT**.
5. Copy the campaign URL and scripts, then click **Done**.

The **TRACKING & PATTERNPILOT** tab is only available after the campaign is saved.

### Redirect vs Direct

| Method | How it works | URL you use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Redirect** | Visitors pass through ClickPattern's tracking server, then go to your lander or offer. | `https://{domain}/c/{campaignId}?…` |
| **Direct** | Tracking runs by JavaScript on your page. No redirect. | Your lander or offer URL with `cpid={campaignId}` added |

For **Direct**, add the tracking script shown on the **TRACKING & PATTERNPILOT** tab to your lander or offer page. Use the campaign URL in every ad, post and link you want tracked: traffic that never carries its cpid is not counted. The script can also read the campaign ID from a cookie an earlier tagged visit left, for up to 30 days.

### Conversion reporting to traffic source

This section on the **GENERAL** tab controls where conversions are reported for this campaign. Each setting has **Default** (use the traffic source's setting) and **Unique for this campaign**.

| Setting | With Unique for this campaign on |
| --- | --- |
| **Traffic source postback URL** | Enter a traffic source postback URL for this campaign only. It replaces the traffic source's URL. |
| **Traffic source postback URL per event type** | Add one row per event type (**1 - install**, **2 - download**, **3 - sell**) with its own URL. Click **Add event** for more rows. |
| **Pixel redirect URL** | Enter a pixel redirect URL for client-side conversion reporting. |

Each URL box has a token picker. Leave the switches off to use the traffic source's settings.

### DESTINATION tab

**Campaign destination** has two modes.

**Flow.** Pick a saved flow in **Select a flow**. Confirm with **Use flow**. A preview shows the flow's default transition, paths and rules. The campaign references the flow, so edits to the flow apply to every campaign using it.

**Path.** The inline flow editor opens. It works like the flow editor in **Create a flow**:

- **Traffic distribution AI (paths)**, **Rule-based paths** with **Add rule**, and **Default paths** with **Add path**.
- Each path has a **Path name**, **Path Destination** (Landers & Offers or Offers only), landers, offers and custom weights.
- Click a rule to edit its **Rule name** and **Conditions**.

### Load from flow template

1. In Path mode, click **Load from flow template**.
2. Pick a flow in **Select a flow**.
3. Confirm with **Load template**. Your current paths are replaced with the flow's paths.

A chip shows **From: \{flow name\}**. If you change the loaded paths and save without **Save as flow**, the source flow is updated too. That affects every campaign using it.

### Save as flow

In Path mode, tick **Save as flow** at the bottom and enter a **Flow name**. Saving creates a new flow from your paths. The campaign then uses the new flow, and the template flow stays unchanged.

If you build paths without a template and don't tick **Save as flow**, ClickPattern still saves them as a flow named `{campaign name} Flow`.

### FAQ

**I changed a template's paths and another campaign changed too. Why?** You saved without **Save as flow**, so the source flow was updated. Tick **Save as flow** to keep the original unchanged.

**Where are Meta Refresh and Double Meta Refresh in the Advanced form?** The Advanced form offers Redirect (302) or Direct. For meta refresh options, set them in a flow and pick it in Flow mode.

**Does "Unique for this campaign" change the traffic source for other campaigns?** No. It applies to this campaign only.
