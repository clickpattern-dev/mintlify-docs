---
title: "Add a lander"
description: "A lander is your pre-sell page between the ad and the offer. You add its URL, choose how many CTAs it has, and put ClickPattern's click URLs and script on the page."
---

### Steps

1. Go to **Performance → Landers** and click **Create**.
2. In **New lander**, pick a **Workspace** and, optionally, a **Country label**.
3. Enter a **Name** and, optionally, **Tags - optional**.
4. Enter the **Lander URL**.
5. Under **Landing page setup**, set **Number of CTAs**.
6. Pick a **Tracking domain**. Use the same domain as the campaign URL.
7. Copy each **Click URL(s)** into the `href` of your CTA links.
8. Copy the **Lander Tracking Script** into your page.
9. Click **Verify** to check the page responds.
10. Click **Save**.

### Field reference

| Field | What to enter | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Workspace** | Owning workspace | Required. |
| **Country label - optional** | A country for naming | Added to the front of the name. |
| **Name** | A unique name | Starts as `Global - `. |
| **Tags - optional** | Labels for filtering |  |
| **Lander URL** | Your page's address, for example `https://www.lander-url.com` | Tokens are optional. Use them for dynamic text on the page. |
| **Number of CTAs** | 1 to 10 | Default 1. Use more than 1 for multi-offer landers. |
| **Tracking domain** | Domain for click URLs and the script | Must match your campaign URL's domain. |

### Click URLs

The click URLs depend on **Number of CTAs**:

| Number of CTAs | Click URLs |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | `https://{domain}/click` |
| 2 to 10 | `https://{domain}/click/1` up to `https://{domain}/click/N` |

Put each one inside the `<a>` tag of a CTA, for example `<a href="https://{domain}/click">Get the offer</a>`.

### Lander tracking script

The **Lander Tracking Script** box has two versions:

| Option | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| **Inline** (default) | Your CTAs are above the fold. It runs immediately with no extra request. |
| **External** | You prefer a one-line tag: `<script async src="https://{domain}/l/script.js"></script>` |

The script rewrites the `/click` links on your page so each CTA click is tied to the visit. The visit itself is recorded when the visitor arrives through the campaign URL.

Only visitors who came through a campaign URL are tracked, plus their clicks on a return visit within 24 hours.

### Setup verification

Click **Verify** to test the **Lander URL**. The result box shows the outcome:

- Success: "URL is accessible and responding correctly".
- Failure, for example "URL returned 404 Not Found" or "Verification failed: Unable to reach the URL".

Verify checks that the page loads. It doesn't load your CTAs, so click through a test visit as well.

### FAQ

**Why do my CTA clicks say "Click tracking information not found"?** The visitor didn't come through the campaign URL, or the script is missing. Open the page through the campaign URL and check that the script is on it.

**What does "This link has expired" mean?** The lander visit is older than 24 hours. The visitor has to come back through the campaign URL.

**Can I use a different domain for click URLs?** No. Use the same tracking domain as the campaign URL, or the click can't be linked to the visit.
