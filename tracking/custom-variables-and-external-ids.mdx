### Where to set them up

1. Go to **Performance → Traffic Sources** and open the traffic source with **Edit**, or click **Create**.
2. Find the **Parameters** section. It has two tables: **External IDs** and **Other Parameters**.
3. Fill in each row and click **Save**.

Templates fill most rows for you. Check them against your traffic source's macro list.

### Columns in both tables

| Column | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Set for you: External ID 1–3, Cost, Variable 1–10 |
| **TS Parameter** | The parameter name in your link, such as `zone` |
| **TS Token** | The traffic source's macro, such as `{zoneid}` |
| **CP Token** | Set for you: the ClickPattern slot, such as `{var1}` |

ClickPattern adds each row to your campaign URL as `parameter=token`, for example `zone={zoneid}`. The traffic source fills in the macro on every click.

### External IDs

You get up to three slots: `{externalid}`, `{externalid2}` and `{externalid3}`. Use them for the traffic source's own click ID, which it needs when you send conversions back.

- Click **Add external ID** to add a slot. Only the last row can be removed, so External ID 1 stays the primary.
- External IDs have no enable switch: a row that exists is active.

### Other parameters

- **Cost** is always on. Map it to the traffic source's cost or bid macro.
- **Variable 1** to **Variable 10** are custom variables. Tick **Enable** for each one you use. Click **Add parameter** to add more, up to 10.

Each variable becomes a report column: group by **V1** to **V10** under Campaigns or Traffic Sources. You can also use **Custom Variable 1–10** in flow rules.

### Click IDs captured automatically

ClickPattern stores these ad platform click IDs even if your template doesn't list them. They fill empty external ID slots in this order:

`fbclid`, `gclid`, `gbraid`, `wbraid`, `ttclid`, `twclid`, `li_fat_id`, `epik`, `msclkid`, `ScCid`, `dclid`, `oppref`, `tblci`, `dicbo`, `obclid`

A value you mapped yourself always wins. A slot that your template reserves for one click ID, such as the `gclid` slot on the Google Ads template, only ever holds that ID.

### Unfilled macros are dropped

Sometimes a traffic source sends a macro without filling it in, such as `zone={zoneid}`. ClickPattern doesn't store these values. The same goes for placeholder words such as `OPTIONAL` or `REPLACE`. Your reports show the variable as empty instead of a fake value.

### FAQ

**How many custom variables can I use?** Ten per traffic source: Variable 1 to Variable 10.

**Why is a variable column empty in my report?** Check that the row is enabled and that the traffic source fills the macro. A macro that arrives unfilled is dropped.

**How do I pass a variable on to my offer?** Use the parameter's own name in the offer URL, such as `{var:zone}` or `{zone}`.
