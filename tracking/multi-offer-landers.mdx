### When to use it

Use it for comparison pages, top-5 lists or quizzes where each button leads to a different offer.

### Steps

1. Create or edit the lander and set **Number of CTAs** to the number of offer buttons (2 to 10).
2. Copy the numbered click URLs into your buttons: **CTA 1** uses `/click/1`, **CTA 2** uses `/click/2`, and so on.
3. In the campaign or flow path, add the offers in the order that matches your buttons.
4. Save the campaign. The **Tracking & PatternPilot** tab lists the CTA links for that campaign.

### How offers are picked

| Click URL | Offer |
| --- | --- |
| `/click` | One offer picked by the path's offer weights |
| `/click/1` | The 1st offer in the path |
| `/click/2` | The 2nd offer in the path |
| `/click/N` | The Nth offer in the path |

- In a flow, "the path" is the path the visitor was sent down.
- Numbers wrap around. With 3 offers, `/click/4` goes to offer 1 and `/click/5` to offer 2.
- Offer caps still apply. A capped offer sends the click to its redirect offer.
- The visitor's lander visit stays valid for 24 hours.

### FAQ

**Do offer weights matter on `/click/N` links?** No. Numbered links always pick by position. Weights only apply to the plain `/click` link.

**I removed an offer and the buttons now go to the wrong place. Why?** Numbers follow the offer order in the path. Removing an offer shifts the ones after it. Check the order after any change.

**Can I mix `/click` and `/click/1` on one page?** Yes. For example, a header button can use `/click` for weighted rotation, while list buttons use numbered links.
