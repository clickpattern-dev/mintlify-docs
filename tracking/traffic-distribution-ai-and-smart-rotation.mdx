### Where to turn it on

| Level | Control | What it optimizes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flow | **Traffic distribution AI (paths)** | Turns on optimization for all rules and default paths at once |
| Rule | Rule switch (**Optimization enabled**) and method | The paths inside that rule |
| Default paths | Switch and method under **Default paths** | The default paths |
| Path | **Traffic distribution AI (offers & landers)** → **Weight optimization** | Landers and offers inside that path |

The flow-level label shows a count such as **2/3 sets of paths optimized**. If you turn off every rule and default-path switch, the flow switch turns off too.

### Methods

| Method | Optimizes for |
| --- | --- |
| **Auto** (default) | ROI when cost and revenue exist, EPV when only revenue exists, otherwise CV |
| **ROI** | (Revenue − Cost) / Cost |
| **EPV** | Revenue per visit |
| **CV** | Conversions per visit |

### Path targets

With **Weight optimization** on, pick what to optimize inside the path:

| Target | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Offers** | Offers only. The only choice for Offers only paths. |
| **Landers** | Landers only |
| **Both separately** | Landers and offers, each on its own |
| **Both combined** (default) | Landers and offers together |

Each lander, offer or path has an **Optimized** checkbox. Clear it to leave that item out of optimization.

### Smart rotation

**Smart rotation** appears next to each optimization switch. If enabled, visitors are automatically sent to a path, lander or offer they haven't seen yet.

### When optimization starts

Until at least one item has 50 visits, ClickPattern uses your manual weights. After that, it picks by performance score.

### FAQ

**Do I still need to set weights?** Yes. Weights drive traffic until there is enough data, and whenever optimization is off.

**Which method should I pick?** Start with **Auto**. Choose **CV** when you track conversions but not revenue.

**Can I keep one offer at a fixed share?** Clear its **Optimized** checkbox. It is then left out of optimization.
