### Open your connections

1. Go to **Settings → MCP Access**. The page is titled **Connect via MCP**.
2. Pick a tab:

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **My connections** | Every connection you've approved, across all workspaces. Each row shows the workspace name. |
| **Selected workspace** | Every connection for the workspace chosen in the workspace selector. Select one workspace to use this tab. |

Each connection shows its name, **Authorized** date and **Last used** date.

### Revoke a connection

1. Find the connection and click **Revoke access**.
2. In the confirmation dialog, click **Revoke access** again.

The connection stops working right away and can't renew itself. Other connections stay connected. To use that client again, connect and approve it again.

You can always revoke your own connections. On the **Selected workspace** tab, members who can manage integrations in that workspace can also revoke other members' connections.

### Limits and rules

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Plan | Available on plans with API access. See [pricing](https://www.clickpattern.com/pricing). |
| Workspaces | One workspace per connection. |
| Rate limit | 30 requests per minute. |
| Permissions | A connection can never do more than your role allows. |
| Write calls | Not idempotent. Check the app before retrying a failed write. |

### FAQ

**I left a workspace. Can I still revoke its connection?** Yes. It stays on the **My connections** tab, marked **Workspace not currently accessible**, and you can still revoke it.

**Why is the Selected workspace tab greyed out?** It needs exactly one workspace. Pick a single workspace in the workspace selector, not **All workspaces**.

**Does revoking remove anything the assistant created?** No. Elements the assistant created stay in your workspace. Revoking only stops future access.
