### Search by name

Type in **Search...** in the toolbar. The table shows only rows whose name contains your text. Search isn't case-sensitive.

### Filter by status

1. On the **ClickPattern Status** column, click the filter icon (**Filter by status**).
2. Tick **Active**, **Paused** and/or **Archived**.
3. Click **Apply**. Click **Clear** to remove the filter.

### Filter a number column

Many number columns have a filter icon, for example **Visits**, **Conversions**, **Cost**, **Revenue**, **Profit**, **ROI** and **ROAS**.

1. Click the filter icon on the column header.
2. Pick **Greater than**, **Equals** or **Lower than**.
3. Enter a value, or use the − and + buttons.
4. For money and rate columns, set **Decimal Accuracy** (0–5) to round values before comparing.
5. Click **Apply**. Click **Reset** to remove the filter.

The filter icon turns highlighted while a filter is on. You can filter several columns at once. A row must match all of them.

### Totals follow your filters

The totals row adds up only the rows that match your search and filters.

### Copy a share link

1. Set up the report: tab, groupings, date range and columns.
2. Click the share icon (**Copy share link**) in the toolbar.
3. The link is copied, and you'll see **Share link copied**.

The link looks like `https://app.clickpattern.com/tracker/v/…` and opens the same report, groupings, date range, time zone, sort and search.

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Who can open it | People signed in to ClickPattern who are members of the workspace |
| How long it works | 90 days |
| Not signed in | They're asked to log in, then taken to the report |
| Not a member, or expired | They see a "not found" page |

Status and number filters aren't kept in the link. Apply them again after opening.

To share with someone outside your team, use a shared report instead. See "[Shared reports](/reports/shared-reports)".

### FAQ

**Why can't my teammate open the link?** They must be a member of the workspace the link was created in. If you had several workspaces selected, the link uses the first one.

**Do filters stay when I change tabs?** Search stays in the address bar. Number and status filters apply to the table you're viewing.
