### Where cost comes from

| Source | How it arrives |
| --- | --- |
| Synced | Pulled from connected ad platforms such as Meta or Google Ads. |
| Estimated | Calculated from your recent cost per visit (CPV) for hours the platform hasn't synced yet. |
| Manual | Set on the campaign, or with **Update Cost**. |
| Token | Passed in the campaign URL by the traffic source. |

### How estimation works

The cost chip on the Dashboard explains it in three steps:

1. ClickPattern calculates your CPV from the last synced period.
2. Estimated cost = CPV × current visits for unsynced hours.
3. Once the platform syncs, estimated cost is replaced with the actual cost.

Estimates cover today and yesterday.

### Include or exclude estimated cost

Reports include estimated cost by default. To use synced cost only, for example to match an invoice:

1. Select a single workspace in the workspace selector.
2. Go to **Data Hub → Integrations**.
3. Turn off **Include estimated cost**.

You'll see **Setting saved** and "Reports now use synced cost only." Turn it back on to include estimates again.

This is a workspace setting. Only workspace Owners and Admins see it. If you view several workspaces at once and any of them excludes estimates, the whole report uses synced cost only.

### Check cost status on the Dashboard

The chip above the KPI cards tells you where the period's cost stands:

- **Cost Synced**: all cost is from your ad platforms.
- **Cost Estimated**: some cost is estimated.
- **Cost Pending**: no cost has arrived yet.

Click the chip to see how many campaigns have synced cost data.

### Sync timing

Connected platforms sync cost on a schedule. The Dashboard tooltip notes that platforms typically sync every 2–6 hours. Check **Logs → Cost Logs** to see each sync, its status and how much spend it imported.

### Why today's cost may change

- An estimate is replaced by the real spend when the platform syncs.
- Ad platforms can update recent spend after the fact, and the next sync picks that up.
- Spend that isn't mapped to a campaign doesn't appear in campaign cost. Watch for the **…% of ad spend unmapped** chip on the Dashboard.

### FAQ

**Why is today's ROI different an hour later?** Estimated cost was replaced with synced cost, or a new sync arrived.

**Why is cost $0 for a campaign?** The platform may not have synced yet, the spend may be unmapped, or the campaign doesn't track cost. See "[Cost not pulling](/troubleshooting/cost-not-pulling)".

**Does estimated cost affect manual cost?** No. Estimates apply to cost from connected platforms.
