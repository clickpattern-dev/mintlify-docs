### Build a grouped report

1. Open **Performance**.
2. In the first group menu, choose the main grouping. It starts on the tab you're on.
3. Choose **Group 2**, then optionally **Group 3** and **Group 4**.
4. Click **Generate**. The button appears once your groupings differ from the default.
5. Use **Table** or **Drill** to change how the result is shown.

Each grouping can be used once. To remove a grouping, click the **×** on the last group you set.

### Table vs Drill

| View | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| **Table** | One flat row per combination, with a column for each grouping. |
| **Drill** | A tree. Expand a row to see the next grouping under it. |

### First grouping options

| Category | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Entities | Campaigns, Offers, Landers, Flows, Traffic Sources, Affiliate Networks, Conversions |
| Tracking | Click ID, Transaction ID, External ID, Postback Param 1–5 |
| Geo | Country |
| Time | Hour of Day, Day of Week, Day, Month |
| Device | Device Type, Brand, Model |
| Network | Connection Type, ISP / Carrier, Mobile Carrier, Proxy |
| Software | OS, OS Version, Browser, Browser Version |
| Other | Traffic Type, Tracking Domain |

Inside a campaign's detail tab, the first menu also lists **V1**–**V10**. If the campaign's traffic source names its variables, you'll see those names instead.

### Extra options for Groups 2–4

Groups 2–4 can also offer **Paths**, **V1**–**V10**, **State / Region**, **City**, **Language**, **Referrer** and **Referrer Domain**. Which ones appear depends on the first grouping.

### What you can combine

The options in Groups 2–4 depend on your first grouping:

| First grouping | Groups 2–4 |
| --- | --- |
| **Campaigns** | Other entities, Conversions, V1–V10, Paths, Referrer, Referrer Domain, plus geo, time, device, network and software options. City isn't offered. |
| **Traffic Sources** | Other entities, V1–V10, Paths, Referrer Domain, plus geo, time, device, network and software options. City isn't offered. |
| **Offers** | Campaigns, Landers, Flows, Traffic Sources, Conversions, plus geo (including City), time, device, network and software options. |
| **Landers**, **Flows**, **Affiliate Networks** | Other entities plus geo (including City), time, device, network and software options. Flows and Affiliate Networks also offer Paths. |
| **Conversions** | Entities, geo, time, device, network and software options, Traffic Type and Tracking Domain. |
| **Click ID**, **Transaction ID**, **External ID**, **Postback Param 1–5** | Group 2 is always **Conversions**. Group 3 can be most other reports. Group 4 is off. |
| **Tracking Domain** | Group 2 is **Campaigns** only. Groups 3 and 4 are off. |
| Any other report (Country, Device Type, OS…) | Entities, geo, time, device, network and software options, Traffic Type and Tracking Domain. |

Whether you can use **Group 4** depends on your plan. A lock icon shows when it isn't included. See [pricing](https://www.clickpattern.com/pricing).

### Detail tabs

Double-click a row to open a detail tab for it. The tab strip above the toolbar keeps your open tabs, so you can jump between them.

| You double-click | The detail tab opens on |
| --- | --- |
| A campaign | Offers |
| An offer | Landers |
| A lander, traffic source, flow or affiliate network | Campaigns |
| A country, device, OS or similar row | Campaigns |

Inside a detail tab:

- The report tabs show the other reports for that item. Campaign and flow tabs also add a **Paths** tab.
- **Edit Campaign** (or the matching item name) edits the item that owns the tab.
- Click **Home** to go back to the main report.
- Click the **×** on a tab to close it. When there are too many tabs, they collapse into one button with a count. Open it and choose **Close all tabs** to clear them.

### Favorites

Save a detail tab to open it again later.

1. Click the star on the tab (**Add to favorites**).
2. Enter a **Favorite name** in **Save as Favorite** and click **Save**.
3. To open it later, click the star next to **Performance** in the sidebar.

Favorites are saved in your browser, so they don't follow you to other devices.

### FAQ

**Why is an option missing from Group 2?** It doesn't apply to your first grouping, or it's already used in another group.

**Can I group by rule?** Not in Performance. Scheduled reports can group by **Rule**.

**Why does Group 3 stay greyed out?** Some first groupings turn it off, such as **Tracking Domain**.
