### Open the scheduler

Use any of these:

- In Performance, click **Export/import → Advanced Report** to start a new report.
- Click **Export/import → Manage scheduled reports** to see your list.
- Click **Advanced**, then **Preview** under **Scheduled Reports for Data Visualisation**.

On **Scheduled Reports**, click **Create Report**. The page lists your scheduled reports across all workspaces.

### Step 1: Report Details

| Field | What to enter | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Report Name** | A name, for example "Daily Campaign Performance" | Required, 2–100 characters |
| **Description** | What the report covers | Optional, up to 500 characters |

### Step 2: Schedule

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Frequency** | **Hourly**, **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly** or **Custom (cron)** |
| **Time of Day** | When it runs, in the selected time zone. Default `08:00`. Daily, Weekly and Monthly only. |
| **Timezone** | The zone the schedule runs in. Defaults to your browser's zone. |
| **Day of Week** | Weekly only |
| **Day of Month** | Monthly only, 1–28 |
| **Cron Expression** | Custom only. Standard 5-field cron, evaluated in the selected time zone. |

Hourly reports run at minute 0 of every hour.

Cron examples from the wizard:

| Expression | Runs |
| --- | --- |
| `0 9 * * 1-5` | 9am every weekday |
| `0 */2 * * *` | Every 2 hours on the hour |
| `30 14 1,15 * *` | 2:30pm on the 1st and 15th of each month |
| `0 0 * * 0` | Midnight every Sunday |

A cron schedule can't run more often than every 15 minutes.

### Step 3: Content

| Field | What to enter | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Date Range** | **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 7 Days**, **Last 14 Days**, **Last 30 Days**, **Last 90 Days**, **This Week**, **Last Week**, **This Month** or **Last Month** | Default **Yesterday** |
| **Workspaces** | The workspaces to include. Their data is combined into one report. | 1–25 |
| **Groupings** | Entities (Campaign, Offer, Lander, Traffic Source, Affiliate Network, Flow, Rule), Geo/Device (Country, Region / State, City, Device, OS, Browser and more) or Time (Day, Month, Day of Week, Hour of Day) | 1–4 |
| **Columns** | Tick columns by group: Traffic, Financial, Rates, Cost Metrics, Revenue Metrics, Platform Data, Events, Quality, Refunds | At least 1 |
| **Sort By** | A column, then **ASC** or **DESC** | Optional |
| **Filters** | A dimension (such as Campaign, Offer, Country or Device) and an operator: **Equals**, **Does not equal**, **Contains**, **Is one of**, **Is not one of** | Optional |
| **Maximum Rows** | How many rows to include | 1–100,000, default 50,000 |

Optional switches:

- **Include empty rows (rows with all zero values)**
- **Compare to previous period (embed Δ% in the email body)**

### Step 4: Delivery

**Report Format:**

| Format | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **CSV** | Works with Excel and Google Sheets |
| **Excel (XLSX)** | Native workbook with a frozen header |
| **PDF** | Print-ready. Capped at 5,000 rows |
| **JSON** | For programmatic use |
| **XML** | For older systems |

**Delivery channels:**

- **Email Recipients**: type an address and press Enter. Up to 10 recipients. At least one is required.
- **BI / Data Visualization**: automatic. Every report gets a permanent link. See below.
- **Webhook URL (optional)**: ClickPattern sends a POST with report details after each run. Works with Zapier, Make.com, n8n or any webhook endpoint.

### Step 5: Review

Check the summary, then click **Create Report**.

### Manage your reports

The list shows **Name**, **Status**, **Schedule**, **Groupings**, **Format**, **Last Run** and **Runs**. Click the menu on a row for:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **View details** | Shows the setup, run history and the BI link |
| **Edit** | Opens the wizard with your settings |
| **Run now** | Runs the report immediately |
| **Download last report** | Downloads the latest file |
| **Pause** / **Resume** | Stops or restarts the schedule |
| **Delete** | Removes the report and its run history. This can't be undone. |

### Connect a BI tool

1. Choose **CSV** as the format and create the report.
2. Run it once with **Run now**, and make sure the date range has data.
3. Open **View details** and find **Persistent URL (for BI Tools)**.
4. Copy the link, or click **Copy Google Sheets formula** to get `=IMPORTDATA("…")`.

The URL never changes, and it always returns the latest run. In Power BI, use Get Data → Web, paste the URL and choose Anonymous. In Looker Studio or Tableau, add it as a CSV or web data source.

### One-time Big Data report

For a single large export:

1. Click **Advanced** in Performance.
2. Under **Big Data Report**, click **Select**.
3. Complete **Report Details**, **Content**, **Delivery** and **Review**. There's no Schedule step.

It runs once right away, and the download link is emailed when it's ready. It's available on the same plans as scheduled reports.

### FAQ

**My cron expression was rejected. Why?** It must be a valid 5-field expression, at least 15 minutes apart. For example, `*/15 * * * *` or `0 * * * *`.

**Why is my PDF missing rows?** PDF stops at 5,000 rows. Use CSV or XLSX for larger reports.

**Who sees my scheduled reports?** The list shows the reports you created.
