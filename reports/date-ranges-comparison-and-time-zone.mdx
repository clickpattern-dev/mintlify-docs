### Choose a date range

1. Click the date range button in the toolbar.
2. Click a preset on the left: **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 7 Days**, **Last 30 Days**, **This Month**, **Last Month**, **This Year**, **Last Year** or **Custom**.
3. For **Custom**, click the **From** and **To** fields and pick days in the two-month calendar.
4. Click **Apply**.

When Compare is off, clicking a preset applies it right away.

The button shows the preset and the dates, for example `Last 7 Days | Oct 18 - Oct 24, 2026`.

### Move to the previous or next period

Click the left or right arrow next to the date button.

| Range | Arrows move by |
| --- | --- |
| Today, Yesterday | 1 day |
| Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days | 7 or 30 days |
| This Month, Last Month, or a custom full month | 1 month |
| This Year, Last Year, or a custom full year | 1 year |
| Other custom ranges | The length of the range |

The right arrow is disabled once the range reaches today.

### Compare two periods

1. Open the date picker and turn on **Compare**.
2. Under **Compare To**, pick **Previous Period**, **Previous Year** or **Custom**.
3. For **Custom**, set **Compare From** and **Compare To**.
4. Click **Apply**.

| Option | Compares with |
| --- | --- |
| **Previous Period** | The same number of days, ending the day before your range starts |
| **Previous Year** | The same dates one year earlier |
| **Custom** | Any dates you choose |

With Compare on, the button shows both ranges, for example `Last 7 Days | … vs …`. In Performance, each metric gets a **Chg** column with the change. On the Dashboard, the trend chart adds "(prev)" lines.

### View in another time zone

Reports use your **Default report time zone** from **Settings → General settings**. To check numbers in another zone without changing that setting:

1. Open the date picker.
2. In **Timezone**, search for a zone and select it.
3. Click **Apply**.

The button adds the city, for example `· New York`, so you know an override is on. To go back, choose **Use my settings (…)** at the top of the Timezone list.

The time zone decides where each day starts and ends. A different zone can move visits and conversions between days.

### FAQ

**Does the time zone override change my account setting?** No. It applies to the view you're looking at. It's also kept in the share link.

**Why does "Today" look short in the morning?** Today runs from midnight in the selected time zone until now.

**Why doesn't a preset apply when I click it?** With Compare on, presets wait for you to click **Apply**.
