### Public and Private workspaces

|  | Public Workspace | Private workspace |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How you get it | Created automatically with your account | You create it |
| How many | One per account | As many as your plan allows |
| What it's for | Elements shared across all your workspaces | Campaigns and the elements only that workspace uses |
| Delete | Can't be deleted | The Owner can delete it |

Elements in the Public Workspace can be used by campaigns in any Private workspace. Elements in a Private workspace can only be used inside that workspace. A campaign can't use an element from another Private workspace. If you try, you'll see **Workspace Mismatch**.

### Create a workspace

1. Go to **Workspaces** and click **New Workspace**.
2. Enter a **Workspace Name** and, optionally, a **Description (Optional)**.
3. If you belong to an organization, choose where to create it under **Create in**: **Personal — workspace stays with your account**, or your organization.
4. If you picked an organization, choose who to add under **Share with**. The organization owner is always added.
5. Click **Create Workspace**.

New workspaces are always Private.

### Find and open a workspace

On the **Workspaces** page, use **Filter** (**All Workspaces**, **Owned**, **Shared with me**) or **Search workspaces...**. Click a card to open the workspace and see its members, details and counts.

### Switch workspaces

1. Click the workspace selector at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Choose **All workspaces**, or tick one or more workspaces. The Public Workspace has a globe icon.
3. Click **Apply**.

Reports and lists then show data from the selected workspaces.

### Edit workspace details

Owners and Admins can click **Settings** on the workspace page to change the **Workspace Name** and **Description**, then click **Save Changes**.

### Delete a workspace

Only the workspace Owner can delete it. The Public Workspace can't be deleted.

1. Make sure the workspace is empty: no campaigns, offers, landers, flows, traffic sources, integrations, domains, automation rules or scheduled reports. Move or delete them first.
2. Open the workspace and click **Delete Workspace**.
3. Read the warning and click **Delete Permanently**.

If anything is left, you'll see **Cannot Delete Workspace** with a list of **Blocking elements**. Click **Edit** next to an element to open it.

Deletion is permanent. There is no grace period and no restore option.

### FAQ

**Can I turn a Private workspace into the Public Workspace?** No. A workspace's type is fixed when it's created.

**What happens to members when I delete a workspace?** All its members and invitations are removed, along with its settings.

**Why don't I see Delete Workspace?** Only the Owner sees it, and never on the Public Workspace.
