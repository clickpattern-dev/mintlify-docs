### The roles

- **Owner**: the person who created the workspace. There is one Owner per workspace.
- **Admin**: manages the workspace, its settings and its members.
- **Editor**: works on content. You can fine-tune an Editor's permissions per member.
- **Read-only**: views data. No actions.

When you invite someone, you choose **Admin**, **Editor** or **Read-only**. Owner isn't an option in the role picker.

### What each role can do (defaults)

| Action | Owner | Admin | Editor | Read-only |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| View campaigns, elements and reports | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Create, edit and delete campaigns, offers, landers and flows | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Connect and configure ad-platform integrations | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| View PatternPilot and rule logs | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Create, edit and delete PatternPilot rules | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Run PatternPilot rules against live data | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Invite members and see pending invitations | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Remove members and change roles | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Edit workspace name and settings | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Move elements between workspaces | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Delete the workspace | Yes | No | No | No |

Editor defaults can be changed per member. See "[Custom Editor permissions and hidden columns](/team/custom-editor-permissions-and-hidden-columns)".

### Compare roles in the app

In the invite dialog, click **Compare permissions** next to **Assign role** to see every permission side by side.

### Change a member's role

1. Open the workspace from **Workspaces**.
2. In the **Members** table, click the edit icon on the member's row.
3. Choose **Admin**, **Editor** or **Read-only** and click the save icon.

Role changes can take up to a minute to apply. The member sees updated buttons after reloading the page.

### Organization roles

If your workspaces belong to an organization, people also have an organization role:

| Role | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| owner | Everything in the organization |
| admin | Organization settings and members. Can create workspaces in the organization. |
| billing | Billing only. No member management, and no workspace content unless also a workspace member. |
| member | Basic membership. Can create workspaces and see the member list. |

Organization owners and admins can open the organization's workspaces without being invited to each one.

### FAQ

**Can I make someone an Owner?** No. Owner isn't available in the role picker, and the Owner's row can't be edited or removed.

**What happens if I lower someone's role?** It applies within about a minute. Actions they're no longer allowed to do are refused.

**Can I hide revenue from a team member?** Yes. You can hide financial columns from Editors and Read-only members. See "[Custom Editor permissions and hidden columns](/team/custom-editor-permissions-and-hidden-columns)".
