### What can move

| Element | Can move to |
| --- | --- |
| Offers, landers, flows, traffic sources, affiliate networks | The Public Workspace or another Private workspace in your account |
| Campaigns | Another Private workspace only. Campaigns can't move to the Public Workspace. |

You need the Owner or Admin role in both the current workspace and the destination.

### Move one element to the Public Workspace

1. Go to **Performance** and open the tab for the element, for example **Offers**.
2. Select the element and open **Actions**.
3. Click **Move to Public Workspace**.
4. Click **Move to Public Workspace** to confirm.

The element can then be used in campaigns in every workspace in your account. Existing campaigns that use it keep working.

### Move one element to another workspace

1. Select the element and open **Actions**.
2. Click **Move to workspace…**.
3. Choose the **Destination workspace**. Each option is marked **Public** or **Private**.
4. Confirm the move.

If campaigns in other workspaces still use the element, you'll see **Can't move yet** with a list of those campaigns. Update or move those campaigns first, then try again.

### Move several elements at once

1. Select two or more elements of the same type. They don't need to come from the same workspace.
2. Open **Actions** and click **Move N to Public Workspace** or **Move N to workspace…**.
3. Confirm the move.

If some moves fail, the message shows how many moved and how many didn't.

### Move a campaign

1. Go to **Performance → Campaigns**, select the campaign and open **Actions**.
2. Click **Move to workspace…**.
3. Choose a Private **Destination workspace** and click **Move campaign**.

Offers, landers, the flow or the traffic source that the destination workspace can't use are detached during the move. The confirmation lists what was detached. Click **Open campaign editor** to attach replacements from the destination workspace.

### What happens to past data

Moving an offer, lander, flow, traffic source or affiliate network doesn't change your past reports. Past visits and conversions stay with the campaigns that recorded them. The move is also recorded in the audit log of both workspaces.

### FAQ

**Why don't I see the move options?** You need the Owner or Admin role. Elements already in the Public Workspace don't show **Move to Public Workspace**.

**Can an Editor move elements?** No. Moving needs the Owner or Admin role in both workspaces.

**Can I bulk-move campaigns?** No. Campaigns move one at a time.
