### Before you start

The **Security** tab appears in **Settings** when you have the Owner or Admin role in the selected workspace.

### Change your password

1. Go to **Settings → Security**.
2. Click **Edit** next to **Change Password**.
3. Enter your **Current Password**, a **New Password** and **Confirm New Password**.
4. Click **Update Password**.

Password requirements: 8 to 64 characters, with at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number and one special character. The new password must be different from your current one.

### Change your email

1. Go to **Settings → Security**.
2. Click **Edit** next to **Email address**.
3. Enter the **New email address** and your **Current password**.
4. Click **Send verification code**.
5. Enter the 6-digit **Verification code** sent to the new address. The code expires in 30 minutes.
6. Click **Confirm change**.
7. Sign out and sign back in to refresh your session.

### Forgot your password?

On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?** and follow the steps to get a verification code by email.

### FAQ

**I get "This email is already in use". What now?** Another ClickPattern account already uses that address. Choose a different email.

**The code didn't arrive or expired.** Click **Back** and send a new code. Each code is valid for 30 minutes.

**I don't see the Security tab.** Select a workspace where you're an Owner or Admin. To reset your password without the tab, use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.
