---

### Turn on two-factor authentication

1. Go to **Settings → Security**.
2. Switch on the toggle next to **Two-factor authentication**.
3. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app, or type the **Manual entry key**.
4. Enter the **6-digit code** from the app and click **Verify & enable**.
5. Save your 10 recovery codes. Use **Copy** or **Download**, then click **I've saved my codes**.

The recovery codes are shown only once. Each code works one time. The card shows how many recovery codes you have left.

### Sign in with two-factor authentication

1. Sign in with your email and password.
2. Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app.

Lost your phone? Click **Can't access your app? Use a recovery code** and enter one of your saved codes.

### Turn off two-factor authentication

1. Go to **Settings → Security**.
2. Switch off the **Two-factor authentication** toggle.
3. Enter your **Password** and click **Disable 2FA**.

### FAQ

**I used most of my recovery codes. Can I get new ones?** Turn two-factor authentication off and on again. Setup gives you a new set of 10 codes.

**Which apps work?** Any TOTP authenticator app, such as Google Authenticator, 1Password or Authy.
