### Send an invitation

1. Go to **Workspaces** and open the workspace.
2. Click **Invite Member**.
3. Enter the **User's email address**.
4. Under **Assign role**, choose **Admin**, **Editor** or **Read-only**.
5. Under **Manage access rights**, optionally hide columns or adjust Editor permissions.
6. Click **Send Invitation**.

The person receives an email invitation to join the workspace.

### Seats

The top of the dialog shows how many seats your plan includes and how many are in use. Pending invitations use a seat when they're accepted. When all seats are used, **Send Invitation** stays disabled until you remove a member.

### Track invitations

Owners and Admins see **Pending Invitations** on the workspace page, with **Email**, **Role**, **Sent** and **Expires**.

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Resend email** / **Resend** | Sends a new email with a new link and a new 7-day expiry. The old link stops working. |
| **Cancel** | Withdraws the invitation. |

Expired invitations stay in the list with an **Expired** label, so you can resend them.

In the **Members** table, each person shows **Active** or **Pending**.

### Accept an invitation

1. Open the link in the invitation email and sign in with the invited email address.
2. Click **Accept Invitation**, or **Decline**.

You can also accept pending invitations in the app. Go to **Workspaces** and click **Invitations**, then click **Accept** or **Decline**.

| Message | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| This invitation was sent to … You are signed in as … | You're signed in with a different email. Sign in with the invited address. |
| This invitation has expired. | Ask the workspace owner to send a new one. |
| This invitation was revoked by a workspace admin. | The invitation was cancelled. |
| This invitation was already accepted. | You're already a member. |

### FAQ

**How long is an invitation valid?** 7 days. Resending it starts a new 7-day period.

**Who can invite members?** Owners and Admins. You can also allow a specific Editor to invite members by changing their permissions.

**Can I invite someone who already has a pending invitation?** No. Resend the existing invitation instead.
