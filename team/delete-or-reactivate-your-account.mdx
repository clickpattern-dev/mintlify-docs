### Before you start

- You can't delete your account while you're the only owner of a workspace. Delete those workspaces first. Otherwise you'll see: "You are the sole owner of one or more workspaces. Transfer ownership or delete them before deleting your account."
- Deletion removes your profile, notification preferences and workspace memberships.

### Schedule deletion

1. Go to **Settings → Security** and scroll to **Danger zone**.
2. Click **Delete my account…**.
3. Optionally enter a **Reason (optional)**.
4. Enter your **Current password**.
5. Type `DELETE MY ACCOUNT` exactly.
6. Click **Schedule deletion**.

You'll see "Account deletion scheduled. You have 30 days to cancel." The **Danger zone** then shows the deletion date.

### Cancel deletion

- From the app: go to **Settings → Security** and click **Cancel deletion** in the **Danger zone**.
- When you sign in: a **Welcome back** page shows your deletion date. Click **Reactivate my account**, or **Keep deletion scheduled** to continue to the app without cancelling.

### FAQ

**Can I still use ClickPattern during the 30 days?** Yes. Choosing **Keep deletion scheduled** takes you to the dashboard. The deletion still happens on the scheduled date.

**What happens after 30 days?** Your account and its data are permanently deleted. This can't be undone.

**Does deleting my account delete my workspaces?** No. You must delete workspaces you solely own before you can schedule deletion.
