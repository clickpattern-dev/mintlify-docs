### View your sessions

Go to **Settings → Security** and scroll to **Active sessions**. Each session shows:

- The device, or the browser and operating system.
- The IP address.
- When it was last active and when it signed in.

Your current session is marked **This device**.

</Frame>

### Sign out a device

- To sign out one device, click the revoke icon on its row.
- To sign out everywhere else, click **Sign out of all other devices**.

You can't revoke the current session from this list. To sign out of this device, use **Log out** in the account menu.

### FAQ

**I see a session I don't recognize. What should I do?** Revoke it, change your password, and turn on two-factor authentication.

**Does signing out other devices also sign me out?** No. Your current session stays signed in.
