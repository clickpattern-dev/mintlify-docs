### Financial

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Cost** | Ad spend. |
| **Revenue** | Conversion payouts. |
| **Profit** | Revenue minus cost. |
| **ROI** | Profit as a % of cost. |
| **ROAS** | Revenue divided by cost, as a ratio. A value of `2` means $2 back per $1 spent. |

### Performance

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Conversions** | Number of conversions. |
| **Click-Through Rate (CTR)** | Clicks as a % of visits. |
| **Conversion Rate (CR)** | Conversions as a % of clicks. |
| **Clicks** | Clicks from your lander to the offer. |
| **Impressions** | Ad impressions. |
| **Conversion Rate per Visit (CV%)** | Conversions as a % of visits. |
| **Impression CTR (iCTR)** | Visits as a % of impressions. |
| **Custom Conversions** | Counts all custom-event conversions, whatever their include-in-conversions setting. |

### Cost Efficiency

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Cost per Visit (CPV)** | Cost divided by visits. |
| **eCPM** | Cost per 1,000 impressions. |

### Revenue Efficiency

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **RPM (Revenue per 1k Impressions)** | Revenue per 1,000 impressions. |

### Engagement

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Cost per Click (CPC)** | Cost divided by clicks. |
| **Cost per Acquisition (CPA)** | Cost divided by conversions. |
| **Earnings per Click (EPC)** | Revenue divided by clicks. |
| **Earnings per Visit (EPV)** | Revenue divided by visits. |
| **Visits** | Number of visits. |
| **Hours Since Conversion** | Hours since the last conversion. |

### Traffic

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Unique Visits** | Visits counted once per visitor. |
| **Unique Clicks** | Clicks counted once per visitor. |

### Quality

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Suspicious Visits (enforced)** | Visits whose fraud score crossed the enforcement threshold. |
| **Suspicious Visit Rate (%, enforced)** | Enforced suspicious visits as a % of visits. |

Quality metrics appear only if your plan includes the Anti-Fraud Kit. They use the enforced ("Blocked") count. The tracker's **Suspicious Visits** column shows the broader flagged count, so its numbers are higher. Set your thresholds with that in mind.

### FAQ

**Why don't I see the Quality group?** Your plan doesn't include the Anti-Fraud Kit. See [pricing](https://www.clickpattern.com/pricing).

**Why is my suspicious-visits rule not firing when the tracker shows many suspicious visits?** The rule uses the enforced count, which is lower than the flagged count in the tracker. Lower your threshold.

**Is ROAS a percentage?** No. ROAS is a ratio. Enter `2` for 2×, not `200`.
