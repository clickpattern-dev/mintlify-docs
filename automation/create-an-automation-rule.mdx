### Step 1: Name the rule

1. Go to **PatternPilot** and click **Create Rule**.
2. Enter a **Rule Name**, for example "Pause unprofitable campaigns".
3. Optionally enter a **Description (optional)** so teammates know what the rule is for.

| Field | What to enter | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rule Name** | A name you'll recognize in the rules list. | Required. Under 200 characters. |
| **Description (optional)** | What the rule does and why. | Under 1,000 characters. |

### Step 2: Choose the target level

Under **Create rule for**, pick the level the rule acts on:

| Option | The rule acts on |
| --- | --- |
| **Campaign** | Campaigns mapped at campaign level. |
| **Ad Set** | Campaigns mapped at ad set level. The action runs on the mapped ad set. |
| **Ad Group** | Campaigns mapped at ad group level. The action runs on the mapped ad group. |

The campaign list only shows campaigns mapped at the level you pick. Changing the level clears your campaign selection.

### Step 3: Choose which campaigns

Pick one targeting option:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Specific campaigns** | Select specific campaigns to target. Search and tick each one (up to 20). |
| **All from traffic source** | Apply to all campaigns from a traffic source. Choose the **Traffic Source**. |
| **All from integration** | Apply to all campaigns from a specific integration. Choose the **Integration**. |

In **Specific campaigns**, each entry shows the campaign name, the platform entity it maps to, and whether it's **Active** or **Inactive**. If nothing is mapped at the chosen level, you'll see a message asking you to map a campaign at that level or pick another target level.

### Step 4: Choose actions

Under **Choose action**, pick an **Action**. Click **Add action** to add more.

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Pause campaign** / **Pause ad set** / **Pause ad group** | Pauses the target on the ad platform. |
| **Resume campaign** / **Resume ad set** / **Resume ad group** | Resumes the target on the ad platform. |
| **Adjust bid** | Changes the bid on the ad platform. |
| **Adjust budget** | Changes the budget on the ad platform. |
| **Send alert** | Alerts you. Nothing changes on the ad platform. |
| **Send notification** | Notifies you. Nothing changes on the ad platform. |
| **Mark** | Marks the target in ClickPattern. Nothing changes on the ad platform. |

A rule can have up to 5 actions. Each action can be used once per rule. A new rule starts with the pause action.

Not every ad platform supports every action. See "[Which platforms support which actions](/automation/which-platforms-support-which-actions)".

### Adjust bid and Adjust budget

These actions add a **Type** field and a **Value** field.

| Type | What **Value** means | Extra field |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Percentage** (default) | The change, in %. Use a negative number to lower, for example `-20`. | **Max bid** or **Max budget** (required) |
| **Fixed** | The new amount, in $. The bid or budget is set to this value. | None |

**Max bid** and **Max budget** are caps. A rule that stays true keeps applying its percentage on every check, so the cap stops the value from growing without limit.

Validation messages:

| Message | Cause |
| --- | --- |
| **Required** (under **Value**) | The value is empty or 0. |
| **Must be above -100%** | A percentage of -100 or lower would set the bid or budget to zero or less. |
| **Must be above $0** | A fixed amount must be greater than 0. |
| **Required cap** | A percentage change needs a **Max bid** or **Max budget** above 0. |

**Next** stays disabled until the name, at least one campaign (or a traffic source or integration) and valid action values are set.

### Step 5: Continue to conditions

Click **Next** to set conditions. See "[Rule conditions, time frames and cooldown](/automation/rule-conditions-time-frames-and-cooldown)". When you're done, click **Create Rule**.

### FAQ

**Why is Next greyed out?** Check the name, the campaign selection and any red fields under **Value** or the max cap. Each must be valid before you can continue.

**Why don't I see some campaigns in the list?** Only mapped campaigns at the selected level appear. Switch between **Campaign**, **Ad Set** and **Ad Group**, or map the campaign first.

**Does a percentage change compound?** Yes, each time the rule acts it applies the percentage to the current value. The required max cap is the upper limit.

**Can I copy an existing rule?** Yes. In the rules list, open the row menu and click **Duplicate**. The wizard opens prefilled as "Copy of …".
