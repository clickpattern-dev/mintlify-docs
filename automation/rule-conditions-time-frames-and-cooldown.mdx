### Time settings

| Setting | What it controls | Options |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Time frame** | How far back to look at data when checking conditions. | **Last 1 hour**, **Last 3 hours**, **Last 6 hours**, **Last 12 hours**, **Last 24 hours** (default), **Last 2 days**, **Last 3 days**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 14 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days** |
| **Check every** | How often the rule is evaluated. | **Every 15 minutes**, **Every 30 minutes**, **Every hour** (default), **Every 2 hours**, **Every 4 hours**, **Every 6 hours**, **Every 8 hours**, **Every 16 hours**, **Every 24 hours**, **Every 48 hours** |
| **Cooldown (minutes)** | After the rule acts, how long before it can act again, even if conditions are still met. | 0 to 1,440. Default 60. 0 means no cooldown. |

**Check every** and **Cooldown (minutes)** are under **Advanced settings**. An info box below always summarizes the setup, for example "Evaluates last 24 hours of data, checks every hour, waits 1 hour after triggering".

### Automatic defaults

When you change **Time frame** with **Advanced settings** closed, ClickPattern sets **Check every** and **Cooldown** for you:

| Time frame | Check every | Cooldown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Last 1 hour | Every 15 minutes | 15 minutes |
| Last 3 hours | Every 15 minutes | 30 minutes |
| Last 6 hours | Every 15 minutes | 30 minutes |
| Last 12 hours | Every hour | 1 hour |
| Last 24 hours | Every hour | 1 hour |
| Last 2 days | Every hour | 1 hour |
| Last 3 days | Every hour | 1 hour |
| Last 7 days | Every 6 hours | 6 hours |
| Last 14 days | Every 6 hours | 6 hours |
| Last 30 days | Every 24 hours | 1 day |
| Last 90 days | Every 24 hours | 1 day |

Open **Advanced settings** to choose your own values. While it's open, changing the time frame no longer resets them.

### Check interval vs time frame

**Check every** can't be longer than the longest condition time frame. For example, checking every 48 hours over the last 24 hours would skip data. You'll see:

> Schedule interval (every 48 hours) exceeds the longest condition time frame (24 hours). Pick a more frequent schedule or a longer time frame.

**Create Rule** stays disabled until you fix it.

### Add conditions

1. Under **Conditions**, set **Match** to **All (AND)** or **Any (OR)**.
2. For each condition, pick a **Metric**, an **Operator**, a **Value** and a **Time frame**.
3. Click **Add condition** for more. A rule can have up to 10 conditions.

| Match | The rule acts when |
| --- | --- |
| **All (AND)** | Every condition is met. |
| **Any (OR)** | At least one condition is met. |

A new rule starts with one condition: Cost > 100 over 24 hours. Each condition has its own **Time frame**. New conditions take the time frame set at the top.

### Operators

| Operator | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **> is greater than** | Above the value. |
| **&lt; is less than** | Below the value. |
| **= equals** | Exactly the value. |
| **≥ is at least** | The value or above. |
| **≤ is at most** | The value or below. |
| **≠ does not equal** | Anything except the value. |

**equals** and **does not equal** rarely match on money, percentage or ratio metrics, because those are almost never an exact number. The wizard warns you when you pick them for such a metric. Use **is greater than** or **is less than** instead.

### Comparison conditions

A comparison condition checks one metric as a percentage of another. For example: "Cost is greater than 50% of Revenue".

1. Click **Add comparison condition**, or click **+ Add comparison (% of another metric)** under an existing condition.
2. Set the **Metric**, **Operator** and **Value** (the percentage).
3. Under **Compare as percentage of:**, pick the **Compare to** metric and its **Time frame**.

Click **Remove comparison** to turn it back into a plain condition.

### Check the summary

The **Rule Summary** box below the conditions restates the rule in plain words: what it applies to, the conditions, the time frame and the actions. Read it before you click **Create Rule**.

### FAQ

**Why didn't my cost rule fire on a 1-hour time frame?** Ad-platform cost arrives by day. Conditions on Cost, Profit, ROI, ROAS, CPC, CPA, CPV or eCPM over a window shorter than 24 hours aren't evaluated. Use **Last 24 hours** or longer for these metrics.

**What happens if a rate metric has no data?** A rate like ROI with zero cost, or CTR with zero visits, can't be calculated. That condition counts as not met.

**What does the cooldown prevent?** Action loops. Without it, a rule whose conditions stay true would act again on every check.
