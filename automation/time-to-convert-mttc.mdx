title: "Time-to-convert (MTTC)"
description: "Time-to-convert (TTC) is how long a visitor takes to convert after clicking your ad. Set Low and High thresholds to spot bot-like instant conversions and slow converters."
---

### Set the thresholds

1. Go to **Data Hub → MTTC**.
2. Enter **Low TTC (below)** and **High TTC (above)** in `HH:MM:SS` format.
3. Click **Save**.

| Field | Default | Allowed range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Low TTC (below)** | `00:00:10` | `00:00:01` to `24:00:00` |
| **High TTC (above)** | `01:00:00` | `00:01:00` to `168:00:00` (7 days) |

**High TTC** must be greater than **Low TTC**. Click **Reset to default** to restore the defaults, or **Cancel** to undo unsaved edits.

Validation messages:

- **Use HH:MM:SS format (e.g. 00:00:10)**
- **Low TTC must be between 00:00:01 and 24:00:00**
- **High TTC must be between 00:01:00 and 168:00:00**
- **High TTC must be strictly greater than Low TTC**

### What the thresholds flag

The colored bar shows three zones:

| Column | Counts conversions | Often means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Low TTC** | Faster than the Low threshold. | Bot traffic. |
| **Optimal TTC** | Between the two thresholds. | Normal behavior. |
| **High TTC** | Slower than the High threshold. | Slow or delayed converters. |
| **MTTC** | Average time-to-convert across conversions. | Your typical conversion delay. |

Add these columns to your Performance reports with **Columns**.

### FAQ

**Do new thresholds change past reports?** Yes. The TTC columns are calculated with your current thresholds each time a report loads, including past date ranges.

**Who can change the thresholds?** Owners and Admins can save. Other roles can view the page.
