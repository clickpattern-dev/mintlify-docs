### When to use it

- Stop spend on campaigns that cost money without converting.
- Raise budgets on campaigns that hit your ROI target.
- Get notified when a metric crosses a threshold.

### How a rule works

Every rule has two parts:

| Part | What it defines |
| --- | --- |
| **Actions** | What the rule targets (campaigns, ad sets or ad groups) and what it does to them. |
| **Conditions** | Which metrics to check, over which time frame, and how often. |

On each check, the rule reads your data for the time frame. If the conditions are met, it runs its actions. A cooldown then stops it from acting again too soon.

### Before you start

A rule can only act on a ClickPattern campaign that is mapped to a campaign on a connected ad platform. If something is missing, **PatternPilot** shows one of two screens instead of your rules:

| Screen | What it means | Button |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Connect an ad integration to use Pattern Pilot** | No ad platform is connected to this workspace yet. | **Connect an integration** |
| **Map your campaigns to use Pattern Pilot** | An ad platform is connected, but no ClickPattern campaign is mapped to one of its campaigns. | **Map campaigns** |

Both buttons open **Data Hub → Integrations**, where you connect a platform (for example Meta or Google Ads) and map your campaigns.

### Open PatternPilot

1. Click **PatternPilot** in the sidebar. The **Automations** page opens.
2. To create a rule, click **Create Rule**. You can also start from **Performance**: click **Create** and choose **PatternPilot Rule**.

A rule belongs to one workspace. Select that workspace in the sidebar before you click **Create Rule**. If several workspaces are selected, the rule is saved to the first one in your list.

### Who can do what

| Task | Owner | Admin | Editor | Read-only |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| See rules | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Create, edit, duplicate, pause, activate and delete rules | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| **Run Rule Now** | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| View rule logs | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

PatternPilot must also be included in your plan. See [pricing](https://www.clickpattern.com/pricing).

### FAQ

**Why do I see "Map your campaigns" when I already connected Meta?** Connecting the platform isn't enough. Each ClickPattern campaign must also be mapped to its ad-platform campaign in **Data Hub → Integrations**.

**Can a rule act on campaigns that aren't mapped?** No. The campaign picker in the rule wizard only lists mapped campaigns.

**Why can't I see the Create Rule button?** Your role doesn't allow managing rules. Ask a workspace Owner or Admin.
