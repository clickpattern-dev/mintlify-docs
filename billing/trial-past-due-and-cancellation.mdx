### Your free trial

New accounts start with a 14-day free trial. Your account is on the Free plan during the trial, and nothing changes automatically when it ends.

The **Billing & Subscription** tab shows the trial:

- During the trial: "You're on a free trial — it ends \{date\}. Upgrade any time to keep growing."
- After it ends: "Your free trial has ended. Choose a plan to unlock more events and features."

Click **Upgrade Now** in **Current Plan** to choose a plan.

### Trial of a paid plan

You can also get a trial of a paid plan. The Billing & Subscription tab then shows **Trial active**: "You're on a \{plan\} trial until \{date\}." The banner also says what happens next: you move to the Free plan, or you return to your previous plan. Click **Learn more** for details. You can close the banner with **×**.

You get an email and an in-app notification before a paid-plan trial ends.

### If a payment fails

When a payment fails, you have 30 days to update your payment method. During those 30 days your account is view-only.

You see:

- A banner at the top of the app: **Payment problem**, "Please update your card by \{date\}. Until then, your workspace is view-only; after the deadline it will move to the free plan."
- On the **Billing & Subscription** tab: **Your billing is past due**, with the date the grace period ends, and a **Past due — grace** chip on **Current Plan**.

You also get an email and an in-app notification when the payment fails, and reminders before the deadline.

To fix it:

1. Click **Update payment** in the banner, or **Update payment method** on the Billing & Subscription tab.
2. Update your card in the Stripe customer portal.

Once the payment goes through, the banners disappear and you can make changes again. If 30 days pass without payment, your account moves to the Free plan. Your data stays.

Team members working in your workspaces see: "Workspace billing issue — please contact your workspace owner to update the payment method before \{date\}."

### What view-only means

Your campaigns keep tracking, and you can still view dashboards and reports. You can't:

- Create campaigns, offers, landers, flows, traffic sources, affiliate networks, domains, integrations or shared reports.
- Create workspaces.
- Create or turn on PatternPilot rules.
- Import conversions.
- Create or run scheduled reports.

If you try, a **Billing past due** message appears with a **Manage billing** button.

### Cancel your subscription

1. Go to **Settings → Billing & Subscription** and click **Manage Subscription**.
2. Cancel your subscription in the Stripe customer portal.

You keep your paid plan's features until the end of the current billing period. Until then, **Current Plan** shows: "Your subscription will be canceled at the end of the current period (\{date\})." Your account then moves to the Free plan.

### FAQ

**Will my campaigns stop if a payment fails?** No. Tracking continues during the 30-day grace period. You just can't create or import new items until the payment goes through.

**Do I lose my data if my account moves to the Free plan?** No. Your data stays. Only your plan's limits and features change.

**Can my team members fix a failed payment?** No. Only the account owner can update the payment method.

**Who do I contact about a billing problem?** Email billing@clickpattern.com.
