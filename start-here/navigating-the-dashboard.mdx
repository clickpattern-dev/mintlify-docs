### The sidebar

| Item | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| Dashboard | See revenue, profit, ROI, conversions and visits at a glance, with trends and top countries |
| Performance | Create and manage campaigns, offers, landers, flows, traffic sources and affiliate networks, and drill into their stats |
| PatternPilot | Create automation rules that pause, resume or adjust campaigns on your ad platforms |
| Data Hub | Connect integrations, upload conversions, set up custom conversion events, traffic filtering and anti-fraud tools |
| Logs | Check received and sent postbacks, cost syncs, errors, filtered traffic and the audit log |
| Workspaces | Create workspaces, manage members and move elements between workspaces |
| Settings | Your profile, preferences, security, domains, tracking URLs, AI connections and notifications (bottom of the sidebar) |

### The header

- **Notifications bell:** alerts such as rule results, integration issues and team invitations.
- **Light/dark toggle:** switch the app's theme.
- **Account menu** ("Hi, your name"): settings and sign out.

At the bottom of the sidebar you'll find the **workspace selector**, where you choose one workspace or **All workspaces** (the globe icon marks your Public Workspace), plus **Help** and **Settings**.

### Performance: where most work happens

The tabs across the top of **Performance** list each type of element: Campaigns, Offers, Landers, Flows, Traffic Sources, Affiliate Networks, Conversions and Country. The Day Parting, Devices and **More reports** buttons open breakdowns by time, device, OS, browser, ISP and more. Select a row to edit, clone, tag or archive it; double-click a row to drill into its stats.

### FAQ

**I don't see some of the items above.** What you see depends on your role in the workspace. Read-only members, for example, can view reports but not create or change elements.
