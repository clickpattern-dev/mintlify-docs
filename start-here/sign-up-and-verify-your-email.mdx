### Create your account

1. Go to [app.clickpattern.com/signup](https://app.clickpattern.com/signup), or click **Get Started** on clickpattern.com.
2. Fill in your first name, last name and email. Middle name, company name and company website are optional.
3. Choose a password and enter it again to confirm. It must be 8–64 characters and include an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and one special character (`! @ # $ % ^ & * ? _ ~ - + = .`).
4. Tick the Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy box, then click Create account.

Prefer not to set a password? Click **Sign up with Google** instead.

### Verify your email

1. Check your inbox for an email from ClickPattern with a 6-digit code.
2. Enter the code on the verification screen.
3. You're taken to the login page. Log in with your email and password. If you were invited to a workspace, you go straight to that workspace instead.

The code expires after 30 minutes. If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder, then click **Resend code**. You can request a new code once every 60 seconds.

### What's created for you

Signing up creates your account, an organization named after your company (or after you, if you leave company blank), and your **Public Workspace**. The Public Workspace holds elements you want to share across your account, such as traffic sources and offers. You can add more workspaces at any time.

### FAQ

**A teammate invited me. What do I do?** Sign up with the email address the invitation was sent to. After you verify your email, ClickPattern adds you to the workspaces you were invited to automatically.

**It says an account already exists.** That email is already registered. Log in instead, or use **Forgot password** to reset it.
