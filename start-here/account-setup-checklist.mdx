### 1. Set your reporting preferences

Go to **Settings → General settings**. Under **Application Preferences**:

Click **Edit** on the Application Preferences card to change them, then save.

| Setting | What to choose |
| --- | --- |
| Default report time zone | The time zone you and your traffic sources report in. Reports group days by this zone. |
| Default currency | The currency you want revenue, cost and profit shown in. |
| Conversion registration time reporting | **Visit timestamp** counts a conversion on the day of the original visit. **Postback timestamp** counts it on the day the conversion arrived. Pick the one that matches how your affiliate networks report. |
| Default main page | Open on **Dashboard** or **Performance** after login. |
| Enable simple campaign form | Keep it on if you're new; the simple form covers most campaigns. |

### 2. Choose a tracking domain

A shared ClickPattern tracking domain is available as soon as you sign up, so you can start right away. For production traffic, add your own domain, such as `track.yourbrand.com`, under **Settings → Domains**. A custom domain is less likely to be blocked and keeps tracking cookies on your own brand.

See: [Add a custom tracking domain](/tracking/add-a-custom-tracking-domain).

### 3. Turn on two-factor authentication

Go to **Settings → Security** and set up two-factor authentication with an authenticator app. Save your recovery codes somewhere safe.

### 4. Plan your workspaces

Workspaces keep campaigns and data separate, for example one per client or per team. Shared building blocks such as traffic sources and offers can live in your Public Workspace so every workspace can use them.

See: [Workspaces: Public and Private](/core-concepts/workspaces-public-and-private).

### 5. Invite your team

Open **Workspaces**, select a workspace and invite teammates as Admin, Editor or Read-only.

See: [Invite team members](/team/invite-team-members).

### FAQ

**Can I change the time zone later?** Yes. Reports use the new zone from then on, and you can also switch time zone on any report without changing the default.

**Which conversion time setting should I use?** If you compare ClickPattern with your affiliate network's dashboard, match the network: most count conversions on the day they happen, which is **Postback timestamp**.
