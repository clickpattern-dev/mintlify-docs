### How ClickPattern fits into your funnel

1. A visitor clicks your ad on a traffic source such as Meta, Google Ads or a native network.
2. The ad's destination is your ClickPattern campaign URL. ClickPattern records a **visit** and gives it a unique click ID.
3. The visitor is sent to your landing page (optional). When they click a call-to-action, ClickPattern records a **click**.
4. The visitor reaches the offer. When they convert, the affiliate network or your store tells ClickPattern, which records a **conversion** against the original click ID.
5. ClickPattern passes the conversion back to the traffic source, by postback or server-side conversion API, so the ad platform learns which clicks paid off.

### What ClickPattern tracks

| Event | Recorded when |
| --- | --- |
| Visit | Someone arrives through your campaign URL or a page with the tracking script |
| Click | A visitor clicks a call-to-action on your landing page |
| Conversion | A postback, pixel, CSV upload or store order confirms a sale, lead or other event |
| Refund | A refund or chargeback arrives; it is recorded as negative revenue, not by editing the original sale |

### Key features

| Feature | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Redirect and direct tracking | Track through a redirect link, or with a script on your page and no redirect |
| Flows | Route visitors to different landers and offers by country, device, time and more |
| Server-side conversions | Send conversions to connected ad platforms through their conversion APIs |
| Automatic cost sync | Pull ad spend from connected platforms so profit and ROI are accurate |
| PatternPilot | Pause, resume or change bids and budgets automatically when metrics cross your thresholds |
| Traffic protection | Filter unwanted traffic and flag suspicious visits with the Anti-Fraud Kit |
| Workspaces and roles | Separate clients or teams and control who can see and change what |
| AI assistant access | Connect Claude, ChatGPT or other AI tools to your data |

### Who uses ClickPattern

- **Affiliate and performance marketers** running paid traffic who need ROI per campaign, offer and placement.
- **Agencies** managing several clients in separate workspaces.
- **E-commerce brands** tracking store orders and refunds back to the ads that drove them.
- **Lead generation teams** tracking form submissions as conversions.

### FAQ

**Do I need to install anything?** No. ClickPattern runs in your browser. You add a tracking link to your ads and, for some setups, a short script to your pages.

**Does it work with any traffic source?** Yes. Any traffic source that accepts a destination URL works. ClickPattern has ready-made templates for common networks, and you can set up any other as a custom traffic source.

**Can I use it without a landing page?** Yes. A campaign can send visitors straight to an offer.
