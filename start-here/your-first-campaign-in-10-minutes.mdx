title: "Your first campaign in 10 minutes"
description: "The simplest working setup: one traffic source, one offer, no landing page, redirect tracking. You'll end with a campaign URL to put in your ad and a postback URL for your affiliate network."
---

### Before you start

- Your offer link from the affiliate network.
- The name of the network's sub ID parameter that carries your click ID back (often `subid`, `aff_sub`, `s2` or `clickid`). Check the network's tracking docs.

### Step 1: Add a traffic source

1. Go to **Performance → Traffic Sources** and click **Create**.
2. Search for your traffic source and select its template, or choose **Custom** if it isn't listed.
3. Check the name and currency, then click **Save**.

Templates fill in the right URL parameters for that network, so visits arrive with campaign, ad and placement details.

### Step 2: Add your offer

1. Go to **Performance → Offers** and click **Create**.
2. Enter a **Name**.
3. Paste your affiliate link into **Offer URL** and add the `{clickid}` token in the network's sub ID parameter, for example: `https://network.example/offer?aff_id=123&aff_sub={clickid}`
4. Leave **Payout type** on **Auto** so the payout comes from the network's postback.
5. Click **Create Offer**.

`{clickid}` is replaced with each visitor's unique click ID. Without it, ClickPattern can't match conversions to visits.

</Frame>

### Step 3: Create the campaign

1. Go to **Performance → Campaigns**, click the arrow next to **Create** and choose **Campaign Simple**.
2. Pick the **Workspace**, a **Country label** (or Global) and a **Campaign name**.
3. Select the **Traffic source** from Step 1.
4. Under the destination, keep **Offers only** and select your offer.
5. Leave **Transition to offer** on **302 Redirect**.
6. Click **Create Campaign**.

### Step 4: Put the campaign URL in your ad

In Performance → Campaigns, select your new campaign, click Edit and open the **Tracking & PatternPilot** tab and copy the **Campaign URL**. It looks like:

`https://{domain}/c/3f9a1c2e-…?…`

Use it as your ad's destination URL. The parameters after `?` come from your traffic source template; keep them.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/shared/campaign-tracking-tab.jpg" alt="Tracking & PatternPilot tab with the Campaign URL" />
</Frame>

### Step 5: Give your network the postback URL

1. Go to **Settings → Tracking URLs** and copy the **Secure postback URL** under **Postback URL**: `https://{domain}/postback?cid=REPLACE&et=OPTIONAL&payout=OPTIONAL&currency=OPTIONAL&txid=OPTIONAL`
2. Replace `REPLACE` with your network's click ID macro (the one that returns the value you put in its sub ID), and replace each `OPTIONAL` with the matching network macro or delete that parameter. Example: `https://{domain}/postback?cid={aff_sub}&payout={payout}&txid={transaction_id}`
3. Paste it into the network's postback or conversion tracking settings.

### Step 6: Test it

Run one test click and one test postback before you spend money. See: Test a campaign before sending traffic.

### FAQ

**Do I need a landing page?** No. This setup sends visitors straight to the offer. Add a lander later with the Landers & Offers destination.

**My network isn't in the affiliate network list. Does that matter?** Not for this setup. The postback URL works with any network that can call a URL when a conversion happens.
