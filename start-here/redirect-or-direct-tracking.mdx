---
title: "Redirect or direct tracking?"
description: "Redirect tracking sends visitors through a ClickPattern link before your page. Direct tracking skips the redirect: your ad links straight to your page, and a script on the page reports the visit. Start with redirect unless you have a reason not to."
---

### How each works

**Redirect:** your ad points to `https://{domain}/c/{campaignId}?…`. ClickPattern records the visit, picks the lander or offer, and forwards the visitor in a fraction of a second.

**Direct:** your ad points to your own page with the campaign ID added, `https://yourpage.com/?cpid={campaignId}&…`. The ClickPattern script on that page records the visit:

```
<script async src="https://{domain}/d/script.js"></script>
```

### Which to choose

|  | Redirect | Direct |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Setup | Paste one URL into your ad | Add the script to every landing page, and add `cpid` to every ad link |
| Page load | One extra hop | No extra hop |
| Ad platforms that reject redirect links | Not suitable | Works |
| Organic, social and email links | Works if you control the link | Works for any link that carries `cpid` |
| Rule-based routing to different landers and offers | Yes | Visitors land on the page you linked |
| Lander protection and honeypot traffic checks | Yes | No |

### Choose redirect if

- You want ClickPattern to choose between landers or offers, or route by country, device or time.
- You run affiliate offers through a lander and want the simplest setup.

### Choose direct if

- Your ad platform doesn't allow redirecting tracking links, or flags them.
- You track your own site or store and want the URL in the ad to be your own domain.
- Page speed matters more than routing.

### Important for direct tracking

Traffic that never carried `cpid` isn't counted. Use the URL from the campaign's tracking tab in every ad, post and link you want tracked, and put the script on every page visitors can land on.

### FAQ

**Can I switch later?** Yes. Change the tracking method on the campaign and update the URL in your ads.

**Does direct tracking still give me a click ID for my network?** Yes. The script assigns a click ID to each visit and passes it on when the visitor clicks through to the offer.
